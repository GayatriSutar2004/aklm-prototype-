Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-05-450-01-06-46960 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Utilities West. The bill workflow supports disconnection and records connection information under the West operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 27 units and a listed service value of 143 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 174 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-05-450-01-06-46960.
