Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-05-450-07-06-53014 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Utilities West. The bill workflow supports disconnection and records service request information under the West operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 77 units and a listed service value of 303 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 74 minutes. For reconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-05-450-07-06-53014.
