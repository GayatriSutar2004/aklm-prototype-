Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-05-450-03-06-48978 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Utilities West. The account workflow supports inspection and records meter information under the West operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 70 units and a listed service value of 56 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 20 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-05-450-03-06-48978.
