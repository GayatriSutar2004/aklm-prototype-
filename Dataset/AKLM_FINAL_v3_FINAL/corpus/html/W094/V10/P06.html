Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-05-450-10-06-56041 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Utilities West. The inspection workflow supports connection and records service request information under the West operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 23 units and a listed service value of 383 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 24 minutes. For billing, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-05-450-10-06-56041.
