Support Procedure

Reference UTI-05-450-02-04-47343 governs the support procedure for Solstice Utilities West. The service request workflow supports disconnection and records bill information under the West operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 15 units and a listed service value of 105 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 195 minutes. For billing, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-05-450-02-04-47343.
