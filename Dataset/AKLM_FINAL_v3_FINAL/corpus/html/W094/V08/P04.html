Support Procedure

Reference UTI-05-450-08-04-53397 governs the support procedure for Solstice Utilities West. The service request workflow supports disconnection and records meter information under the West operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 65 units and a listed service value of 265 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 95 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-05-450-08-04-53397.
