Support Procedure

Reference UTI-05-450-06-04-51379 governs the support procedure for Solstice Utilities West. The inspection workflow supports reconnection and records connection information under the West operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 22 units and a listed service value of 352 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 68 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-05-450-06-04-51379.
