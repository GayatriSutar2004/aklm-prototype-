Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HR-06-267-01-06-21178 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Hr Coastal. The training workflow supports enrolment and records training information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 50 units and a listed service value of 67 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 89 minutes. For transfer, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HR-06-267-01-06-21178.
