Support Procedure

Reference HR-06-267-03-04-22570 governs the support procedure for Harbor Hr Coastal. The leave request workflow supports approval and records leave request information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 20 units and a listed service value of 196 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 33 minutes. For enrolment, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HR-06-267-03-04-22570.
