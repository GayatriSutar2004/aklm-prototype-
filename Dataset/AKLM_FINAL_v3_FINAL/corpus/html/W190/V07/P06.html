Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-10-014-07-06-13238 governs the eligibility and requirements for Aurora Public Service Lakeside. The appointment workflow supports verification and records case information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 84 units and a listed service value of 201 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 98 minutes. For appeal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-10-014-07-06-13238.
