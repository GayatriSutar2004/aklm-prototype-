Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-10-014-03-06-09202 governs the eligibility and requirements for Aurora Public Service Lakeside. The permit workflow supports submission and records permit information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 77 units and a listed service value of 375 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 44 minutes. For appeal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-10-014-03-06-09202.
