Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-10-014-09-06-15256 governs the eligibility and requirements for Aurora Public Service Lakeside. The permit workflow supports submission and records appointment information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 48 units and a listed service value of 114 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 125 minutes. For renewal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-10-014-09-06-15256.
