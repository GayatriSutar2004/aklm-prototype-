Support Procedure

Reference PUB-10-014-01-04-06558 governs the support procedure for Aurora Public Service Lakeside. The application workflow supports appeal and records request information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 40 units and a listed service value of 257 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 115 minutes. For review, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-10-014-01-04-06558.
