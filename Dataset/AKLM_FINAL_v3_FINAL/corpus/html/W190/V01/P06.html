Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-10-014-01-06-07184 governs the eligibility and requirements for Aurora Public Service Lakeside. The appointment workflow supports appeal and records application information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 34 units and a listed service value of 41 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 198 minutes. For appeal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-10-014-01-06-07184.
