Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-10-014-05-06-11220 governs the eligibility and requirements for Aurora Public Service Lakeside. The application workflow supports renewal and records certificate information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 41 units and a listed service value of 288 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 71 minutes. For appeal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-10-014-05-06-11220.
