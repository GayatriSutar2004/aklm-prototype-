Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-10-014-06-06-12229 governs the eligibility and requirements for Aurora Public Service Lakeside. The certificate workflow supports appeal and records request information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 23 units and a listed service value of 455 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 175 minutes. For submission, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-10-014-06-06-12229.
