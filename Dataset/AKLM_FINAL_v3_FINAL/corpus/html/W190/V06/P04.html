Support Procedure

Reference PUB-10-014-06-04-11603 governs the support procedure for Aurora Public Service Lakeside. The case workflow supports renewal and records application information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 29 units and a listed service value of 250 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 92 minutes. For appeal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-10-014-06-04-11603.
