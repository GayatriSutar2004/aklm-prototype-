Timing And Scheduling

Reference PUB-10-014-02-07-08506 governs the timing and scheduling for Aurora Public Service Lakeside. The permit workflow supports submission and records application information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 13 units and a listed service value of 100 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 72 minutes. For renewal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-10-014-02-07-08506.
