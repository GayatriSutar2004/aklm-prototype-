Support Procedure

Reference ECO-01-296-08-04-80282 governs the support procedure for Atlas Ecommerce River. The return workflow supports delivery and records return information under the River operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 30 units and a listed service value of 329 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 119 minutes. For purchase, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-01-296-08-04-80282.
