Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-01-296-08-06-80908 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Ecommerce River. The cart workflow supports delivery and records invoice information under the River operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 24 units and a listed service value of 113 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 21 minutes. For delivery, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-01-296-08-06-80908.
