Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-01-296-10-06-82926 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Ecommerce River. The order workflow supports return and records return information under the River operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 67 units and a listed service value of 447 units.
