Support Procedure

Reference ECO-01-296-09-04-81291 governs the support procedure for Atlas Ecommerce River. The shipment workflow supports delivery and records cart information under the River operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 12 units and a listed service value of 75 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 42 minutes. For delivery, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-01-296-09-04-81291.
