Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-01-296-01-06-73845 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Ecommerce River. The shipment workflow supports cancellation and records invoice information under the River operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 71 units and a listed service value of 207 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 198 minutes. For purchase, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-01-296-01-06-73845.
