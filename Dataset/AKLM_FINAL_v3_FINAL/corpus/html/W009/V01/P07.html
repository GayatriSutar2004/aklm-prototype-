Timing And Scheduling

Reference ECO-01-296-01-07-74158 governs the timing and scheduling for Atlas Ecommerce River. The cart workflow supports cancellation and records shipment information under the River operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 68 units and a listed service value of 99 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 149 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-01-296-01-07-74158.
