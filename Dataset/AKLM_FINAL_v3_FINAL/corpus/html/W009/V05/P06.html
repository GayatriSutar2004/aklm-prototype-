Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-01-296-05-06-77881 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Ecommerce River. The product workflow supports purchase and records order information under the River operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 78 units and a listed service value of 454 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 71 minutes. For purchase, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-01-296-05-06-77881.
