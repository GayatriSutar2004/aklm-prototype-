Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-01-296-02-06-74854 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Ecommerce River. The cart workflow supports delivery and records order information under the River operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 53 units and a listed service value of 374 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 121 minutes. For delivery, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-01-296-02-06-74854.
