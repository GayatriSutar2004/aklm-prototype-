Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-01-296-06-06-78890 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Ecommerce River. The return workflow supports purchase and records product information under the River operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 60 units and a listed service value of 200 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 175 minutes. For delivery, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-01-296-06-06-78890.
