Support Procedure

Reference ECO-01-296-06-04-78264 governs the support procedure for Atlas Ecommerce River. The order workflow supports purchase and records cart information under the River operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 66 units and a listed service value of 416 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 92 minutes. For purchase, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-01-296-06-04-78264.
