Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-08-676-01-06-22100 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Utilities West. The bill workflow supports connection and records account information under the West operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 61 units and a listed service value of 395 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 189 minutes. For billing, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-08-676-01-06-22100.
