Support Procedure

Reference UTI-08-676-01-04-21474 governs the support procedure for Lumen Utilities West. The meter workflow supports inspection and records service request information under the West operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 67 units and a listed service value of 190 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 106 minutes. For reconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-08-676-01-04-21474.
