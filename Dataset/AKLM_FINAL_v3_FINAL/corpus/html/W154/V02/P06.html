Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-08-676-02-06-23109 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Utilities West. The connection workflow supports reconnection and records inspection information under the West operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 43 units and a listed service value of 141 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 112 minutes. For connection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-08-676-02-06-23109.
