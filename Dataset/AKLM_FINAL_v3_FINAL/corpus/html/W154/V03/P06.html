Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-08-676-03-06-24118 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Utilities West. The account workflow supports reconnection and records service request information under the West operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 25 units and a listed service value of 308 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 35 minutes. For billing, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-08-676-03-06-24118.
