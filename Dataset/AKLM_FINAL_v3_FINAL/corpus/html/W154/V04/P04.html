Support Procedure

Reference UTI-08-676-04-04-24501 governs the support procedure for Lumen Utilities West. The connection workflow supports disconnection and records meter information under the West operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 13 units and a listed service value of 270 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 56 minutes. For billing, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-08-676-04-04-24501.
