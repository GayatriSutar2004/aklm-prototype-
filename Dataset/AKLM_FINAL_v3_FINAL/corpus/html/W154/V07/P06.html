Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-08-676-07-06-28154 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Utilities West. The bill workflow supports connection and records bill information under the West operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 32 units and a listed service value of 134 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 89 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-08-676-07-06-28154.
