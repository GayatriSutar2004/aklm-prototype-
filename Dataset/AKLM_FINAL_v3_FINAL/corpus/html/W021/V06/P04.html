Support Procedure

Reference EDU-02-740-06-04-73292 governs the support procedure for Nimbus Education North. The course workflow supports withdrawal and records scholarship information under the North operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 57 units and a listed service value of 298 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 95 minutes. For enrolment, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EDU-02-740-06-04-73292.
