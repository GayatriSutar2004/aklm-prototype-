Support Procedure

Reference EDU-02-740-08-04-75310 governs the support procedure for Nimbus Education North. The assessment workflow supports verification and records module information under the North operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 21 units and a listed service value of 211 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 122 minutes. For enrolment, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EDU-02-740-08-04-75310.
