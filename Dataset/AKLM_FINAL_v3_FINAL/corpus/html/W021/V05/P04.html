Support Procedure

Reference EDU-02-740-05-04-72283 governs the support procedure for Nimbus Education North. The certificate workflow supports withdrawal and records assessment information under the North operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 75 units and a listed service value of 131 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 172 minutes. For attendance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EDU-02-740-05-04-72283.
