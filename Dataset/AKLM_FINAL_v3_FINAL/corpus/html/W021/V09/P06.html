Eligibility And Requirements

Reference EDU-02-740-09-06-76945 governs the eligibility and requirements for Nimbus Education North. The certificate workflow supports attendance and records certificate information under the North operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 76 units and a listed service value of 162 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 128 minutes. For withdrawal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EDU-02-740-09-06-76945.
