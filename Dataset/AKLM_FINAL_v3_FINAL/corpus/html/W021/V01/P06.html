Eligibility And Requirements

Reference EDU-02-740-01-06-68873 governs the eligibility and requirements for Nimbus Education North. The scholarship workflow supports verification and records lab information under the North operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 62 units and a listed service value of 89 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 20 minutes. For enrolment, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EDU-02-740-01-06-68873.
