Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-09-271-06-06-53849 governs the eligibility and requirements for Summit Public Service Lakeside. The request workflow supports appeal and records case information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 38 units and a listed service value of 371 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 170 minutes. For appeal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-09-271-06-06-53849.
