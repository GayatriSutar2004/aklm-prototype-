Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-09-271-03-06-50822 governs the eligibility and requirements for Summit Public Service Lakeside. The application workflow supports review and records application information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 13 units and a listed service value of 291 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 39 minutes. For review, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-09-271-03-06-50822.
