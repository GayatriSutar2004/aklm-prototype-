Support Procedure

Reference EDU-07-452-06-04-65192 governs the support procedure for Pioneer Education North. The assessment workflow supports verification and records course information under the North operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 61 units and a listed service value of 297 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 120 minutes. For enrolment, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EDU-07-452-06-04-65192.
