Support Procedure

Reference EDU-07-452-04-04-63174 governs the support procedure for Pioneer Education North. The course workflow supports withdrawal and records scholarship information under the North operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 18 units and a listed service value of 384 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 93 minutes. For enrolment, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EDU-07-452-04-04-63174.
