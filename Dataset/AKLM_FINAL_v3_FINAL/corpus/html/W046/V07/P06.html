Eligibility And Requirements

Reference FIN-03-668-07-06-72902 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Finance Coastal. The statement workflow supports transfer and records account information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 34 units and a listed service value of 354 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 62 minutes. For reconciliation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-03-668-07-06-72902.
