Eligibility And Requirements

Reference FIN-03-668-06-06-71893 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Finance Coastal. The transfer workflow supports dispute and records payment information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 52 units and a listed service value of 187 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 139 minutes. For dispute, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-03-668-06-06-71893.
