Eligibility And Requirements

Reference FIN-03-668-01-06-66848 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Finance Coastal. The statement workflow supports transfer and records transfer information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 63 units and a listed service value of 194 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 162 minutes. For reconciliation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-03-668-01-06-66848.
