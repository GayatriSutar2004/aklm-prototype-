Support Procedure

Reference PUB-02-076-02-04-40527 governs the support procedure for Nimbus Public Service Lakeside. The case workflow supports verification and records appointment information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 63 units and a listed service value of 173 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 179 minutes. For appeal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-02-076-02-04-40527.
