Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-02-076-04-06-43171 governs the eligibility and requirements for Nimbus Public Service Lakeside. The request workflow supports renewal and records appointment information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 21 units and a listed service value of 291 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 108 minutes. For submission, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-02-076-04-06-43171.
