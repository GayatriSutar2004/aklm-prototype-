Support Procedure

Reference PUB-02-076-08-04-46581 governs the support procedure for Nimbus Public Service Lakeside. The case workflow supports submission and records application information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 34 units and a listed service value of 333 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 79 minutes. For appeal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-02-076-08-04-46581.
