Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-02-076-03-06-42162 governs the eligibility and requirements for Nimbus Public Service Lakeside. The appointment workflow supports review and records application information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 39 units and a listed service value of 124 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 185 minutes. For appeal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-02-076-03-06-42162.
