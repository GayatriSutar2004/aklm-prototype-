Support Procedure

Reference PUB-02-076-06-04-44563 governs the support procedure for Nimbus Public Service Lakeside. The request workflow supports appeal and records request information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 70 units and a listed service value of 420 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 52 minutes. For appeal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-02-076-06-04-44563.
