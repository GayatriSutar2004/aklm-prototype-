Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-02-076-01-06-40144 governs the eligibility and requirements for Nimbus Public Service Lakeside. The application workflow supports verification and records request information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 75 units and a listed service value of 211 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 158 minutes. For appeal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-02-076-01-06-40144.
