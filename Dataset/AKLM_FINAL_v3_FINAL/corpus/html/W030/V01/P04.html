Support Procedure

Reference PUB-02-076-01-04-39518 governs the support procedure for Nimbus Public Service Lakeside. The permit workflow supports verification and records application information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 81 units and a listed service value of 427 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 75 minutes. For review, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-02-076-01-04-39518.
