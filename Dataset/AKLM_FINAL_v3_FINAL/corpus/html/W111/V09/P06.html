Eligibility And Requirements

Reference EVE-06-082-09-06-89655 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Events North. The schedule workflow supports cancellation and records registration information under the North operating profile. For registration, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EVE-06-082-09-06-89655.
