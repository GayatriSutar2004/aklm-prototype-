Eligibility And Requirements

Reference EVE-06-082-02-06-82592 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Events North. The pass workflow supports rescheduling and records registration information under the North operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 16 units and a listed service value of 213 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 56 minutes. For transfer, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EVE-06-082-02-06-82592.
