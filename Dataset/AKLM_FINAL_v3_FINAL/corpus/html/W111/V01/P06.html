Eligibility And Requirements

Reference EVE-06-082-01-06-81583 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Events North. The registration workflow supports registration and records session information under the North operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 34 units and a listed service value of 46 units.
