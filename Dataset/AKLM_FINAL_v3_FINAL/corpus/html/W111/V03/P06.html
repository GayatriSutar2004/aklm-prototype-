Eligibility And Requirements

Reference EVE-06-082-03-06-83601 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Events North. The schedule workflow supports cancellation and records schedule information under the North operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 77 units and a listed service value of 380 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 160 minutes. For rescheduling, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EVE-06-082-03-06-83601.
