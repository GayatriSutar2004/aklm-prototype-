Support Procedure

Reference UTI-03-964-03-04-31592 governs the support procedure for Vertex Utilities West. The meter workflow supports inspection and records service request information under the West operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 27 units and a listed service value of 104 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 108 minutes. For reconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-03-964-03-04-31592.
