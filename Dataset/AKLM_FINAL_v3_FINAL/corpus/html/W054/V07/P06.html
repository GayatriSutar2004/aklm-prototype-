Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-03-964-07-06-36254 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Utilities West. The meter workflow supports billing and records inspection information under the West operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 28 units and a listed service value of 135 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 64 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-03-964-07-06-36254.
