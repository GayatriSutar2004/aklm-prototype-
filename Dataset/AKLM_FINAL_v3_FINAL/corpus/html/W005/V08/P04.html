Support Procedure

Reference HEA-01-148-08-04-48606 governs the support procedure for Atlas Healthcare Central. The appointment workflow supports cancellation and records session information under the Central operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 33 units and a listed service value of 228 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 118 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-01-148-08-04-48606.
