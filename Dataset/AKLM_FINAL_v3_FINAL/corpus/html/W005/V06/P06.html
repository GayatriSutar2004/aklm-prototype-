Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HEA-01-148-06-06-47214 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Healthcare Central. The appointment workflow supports review and records session information under the Central operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 63 units and a listed service value of 99 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 174 minutes. For rescheduling, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-01-148-06-06-47214.
