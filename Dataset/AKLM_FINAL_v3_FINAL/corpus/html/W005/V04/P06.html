Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HEA-01-148-04-06-45196 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Healthcare Central. The care plan workflow supports rescheduling and records care plan information under the Central operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 20 units and a listed service value of 186 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 147 minutes. For rescheduling, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-01-148-04-06-45196.
