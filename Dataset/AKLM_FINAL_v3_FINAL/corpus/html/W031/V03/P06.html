Eligibility And Requirements

Reference EVE-02-113-03-06-50081 governs the eligibility and requirements for Nimbus Events North. The session workflow supports registration and records ticket information under the North operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 58 units and a listed service value of 44 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 140 minutes. For check-in, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EVE-02-113-03-06-50081.
