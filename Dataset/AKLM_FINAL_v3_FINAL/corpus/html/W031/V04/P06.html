Eligibility And Requirements

Reference EVE-02-113-04-06-51090 governs the eligibility and requirements for Nimbus Events North. The venue workflow supports registration and records venue information under the North operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 40 units and a listed service value of 211 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 63 minutes. For rescheduling, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EVE-02-113-04-06-51090.
