Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PRO-07-711-06-06-21251 governs the eligibility and requirements for Pioneer Property Highland. The maintenance request workflow supports maintenance and records maintenance request information under the Highland operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 30 units and a listed service value of 363 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 69 minutes. For renewal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PRO-07-711-06-06-21251.
