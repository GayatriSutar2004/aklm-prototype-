Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-02-814-10-06-93792 governs the eligibility and requirements for Nimbus Travel East. The boarding pass workflow supports transfer and records itinerary information under the East operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 17 units and a listed service value of 169 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 142 minutes. For check-in, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-02-814-10-06-93792.
