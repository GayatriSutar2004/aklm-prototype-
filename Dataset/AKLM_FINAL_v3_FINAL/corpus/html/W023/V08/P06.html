Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-02-814-08-06-91774 governs the eligibility and requirements for Nimbus Travel East. The luggage workflow supports cancellation and records boarding pass information under the East operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 53 units and a listed service value of 256 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 115 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-02-814-08-06-91774.
