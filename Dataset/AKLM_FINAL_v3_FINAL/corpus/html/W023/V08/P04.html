Support Procedure

Reference TRA-02-814-08-04-91148 governs the support procedure for Nimbus Travel East. The reservation workflow supports cancellation and records itinerary information under the East operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 59 units and a listed service value of 51 units. For modification, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-02-814-08-04-91148.
