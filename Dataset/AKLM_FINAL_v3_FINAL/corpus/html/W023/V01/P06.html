Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-02-814-01-06-84711 governs the eligibility and requirements for Nimbus Travel East. The itinerary workflow supports check-in and records transfer information under the East operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 21 units and a listed service value of 350 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 111 minutes. For modification, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-02-814-01-06-84711.
