Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-02-814-04-06-87738 governs the eligibility and requirements for Nimbus Travel East. The boarding pass workflow supports transfer and records transfer information under the East operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 46 units and a listed service value of 430 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 61 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-02-814-04-06-87738.
