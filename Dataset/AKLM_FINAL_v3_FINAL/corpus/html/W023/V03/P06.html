Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-02-814-03-06-86729 governs the eligibility and requirements for Nimbus Travel East. The transfer workflow supports booking and records itinerary information under the East operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 64 units and a listed service value of 263 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 138 minutes. For modification, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-02-814-03-06-86729.
