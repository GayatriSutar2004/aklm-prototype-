Support Procedure

Reference HEA-08-343-10-04-59284 governs the support procedure for Lumen Healthcare Central. The appointment workflow supports verification and records referral information under the Central operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 50 units and a listed service value of 308 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 180 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-08-343-10-04-59284.
