Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HEA-08-343-10-06-59910 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Healthcare Central. The session workflow supports rescheduling and records session information under the Central operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 44 units and a listed service value of 92 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 82 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-08-343-10-06-59910.
