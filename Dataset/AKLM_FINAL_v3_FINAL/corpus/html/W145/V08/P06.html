Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HEA-08-343-08-06-57892 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Healthcare Central. The appointment workflow supports cancellation and records care plan information under the Central operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 80 units and a listed service value of 179 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 55 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-08-343-08-06-57892.
