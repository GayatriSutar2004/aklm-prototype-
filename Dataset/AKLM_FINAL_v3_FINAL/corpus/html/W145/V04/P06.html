Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HEA-08-343-04-06-53856 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Healthcare Central. The session workflow supports rescheduling and records care plan information under the Central operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 73 units and a listed service value of 353 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 182 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-08-343-04-06-53856.
