Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HEA-08-343-06-06-55874 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Healthcare Central. The care plan workflow supports review and records membership information under the Central operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 37 units and a listed service value of 266 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 28 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-08-343-06-06-55874.
