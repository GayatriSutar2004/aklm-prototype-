Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HEA-08-343-05-06-54865 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Healthcare Central. The record workflow supports booking and records referral information under the Central operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 55 units and a listed service value of 99 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 105 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-08-343-05-06-54865.
