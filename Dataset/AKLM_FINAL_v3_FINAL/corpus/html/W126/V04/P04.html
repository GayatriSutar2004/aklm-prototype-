Support Procedure

Reference FIN-07-637-04-04-02769 governs the support procedure for Pioneer Finance Coastal. The transfer workflow supports dispute and records statement information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 34 units and a listed service value of 405 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 49 minutes. For transfer, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-07-637-04-04-02769.
