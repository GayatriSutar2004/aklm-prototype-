Support Procedure

Reference FIN-07-637-10-04-08823 governs the support procedure for Pioneer Finance Coastal. The transfer workflow supports dispute and records transfer information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 84 units and a listed service value of 144 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 130 minutes. For transfer, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-07-637-10-04-08823.
