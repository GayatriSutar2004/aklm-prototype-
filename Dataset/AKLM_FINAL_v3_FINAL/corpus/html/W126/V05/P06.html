Eligibility And Requirements

Reference FIN-07-637-05-06-04404 governs the eligibility and requirements for Pioneer Finance Coastal. The payment workflow supports transfer and records transfer information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 89 units and a listed service value of 356 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 55 minutes. For transfer, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-07-637-05-06-04404.
