Support Procedure

Reference AUT-02-261-02-04-80122 governs the support procedure for Nimbus Automotive Central. The vehicle workflow supports claim and records service booking information under the Central operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 79 units and a listed service value of 194 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 135 minutes. For claim, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-02-261-02-04-80122.
