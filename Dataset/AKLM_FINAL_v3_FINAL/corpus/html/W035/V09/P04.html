Support Procedure

Reference AUT-02-261-09-04-87185 governs the support procedure for Nimbus Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports maintenance and records service booking information under the Central operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 32 units and a listed service value of 100 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 139 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-02-261-09-04-87185.
