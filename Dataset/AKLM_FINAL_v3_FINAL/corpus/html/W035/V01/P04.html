Support Procedure

Reference AUT-02-261-01-04-79113 governs the support procedure for Nimbus Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports inspection and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 18 units and a listed service value of 448 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 31 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-02-261-01-04-79113.
