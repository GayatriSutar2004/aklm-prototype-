Operating Policy

Reference AUT-02-261-01-03-78800 governs the operating policy for Nimbus Automotive Central. The part workflow supports inspection and records service booking information under the Central operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 21 units and a listed service value of 135 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 80 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-02-261-01-03-78800.
