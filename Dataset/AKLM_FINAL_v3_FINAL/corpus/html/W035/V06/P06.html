Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-02-261-06-06-84784 governs the eligibility and requirements for Nimbus Automotive Central. The vehicle workflow supports inspection and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 80 units and a listed service value of 225 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 91 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-02-261-06-06-84784.
