Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-02-261-03-06-81757 governs the eligibility and requirements for Nimbus Automotive Central. The inspection workflow supports maintenance and records vehicle information under the Central operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 55 units and a listed service value of 145 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 141 minutes. For inspection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-02-261-03-06-81757.
