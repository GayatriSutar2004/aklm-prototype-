Support Procedure

Reference AUT-02-261-03-04-81131 governs the support procedure for Nimbus Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports maintenance and records inspection information under the Central operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 61 units and a listed service value of 361 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 58 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-02-261-03-04-81131.
