Operating Policy

Reference AUT-02-261-03-03-80818 governs the operating policy for Nimbus Automotive Central. The vehicle workflow supports maintenance and records part information under the Central operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 64 units and a listed service value of 48 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 107 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-02-261-03-03-80818.
