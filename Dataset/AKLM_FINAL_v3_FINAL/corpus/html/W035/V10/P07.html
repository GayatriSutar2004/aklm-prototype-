Timing And Scheduling

Reference AUT-02-261-10-07-89133 governs the timing and scheduling for Nimbus Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports replacement and records part information under the Central operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 84 units and a listed service value of 364 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 96 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-02-261-10-07-89133.
