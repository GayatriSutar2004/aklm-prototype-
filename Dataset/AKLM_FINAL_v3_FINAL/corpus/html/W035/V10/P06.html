Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-02-261-10-06-88820 governs the eligibility and requirements for Nimbus Automotive Central. The part workflow supports booking and records vehicle information under the Central operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 87 units and a listed service value of 51 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 145 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-02-261-10-06-88820.
