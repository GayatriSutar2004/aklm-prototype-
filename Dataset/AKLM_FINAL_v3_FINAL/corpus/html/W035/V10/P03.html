Operating Policy

Reference AUT-02-261-10-03-87881 governs the operating policy for Nimbus Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports booking and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 17 units and a listed service value of 375 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 111 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-02-261-10-03-87881.
