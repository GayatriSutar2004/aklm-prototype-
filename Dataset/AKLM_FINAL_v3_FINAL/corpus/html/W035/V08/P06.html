Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-02-261-08-06-86802 governs the eligibility and requirements for Nimbus Automotive Central. The warranty workflow supports maintenance and records warranty information under the Central operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 44 units and a listed service value of 138 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 118 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-02-261-08-06-86802.
