Operating Policy

Reference AUT-02-261-04-03-81827 governs the operating policy for Nimbus Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports maintenance and records vehicle information under the Central operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 46 units and a listed service value of 215 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 30 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-02-261-04-03-81827.
