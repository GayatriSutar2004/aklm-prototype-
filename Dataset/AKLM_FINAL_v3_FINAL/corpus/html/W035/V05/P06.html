Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-02-261-05-06-83775 governs the eligibility and requirements for Nimbus Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports replacement and records inspection information under the Central operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 19 units and a listed service value of 58 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 168 minutes. For inspection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-02-261-05-06-83775.
