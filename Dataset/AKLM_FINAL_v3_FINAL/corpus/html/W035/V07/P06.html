Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-02-261-07-06-85793 governs the eligibility and requirements for Nimbus Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports claim and records service booking information under the Central operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 62 units and a listed service value of 392 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 195 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-02-261-07-06-85793.
