Eligibility And Requirements

Reference FOO-06-119-01-06-89502 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Food South. The delivery workflow supports modification and records order information under the South operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 53 units and a listed service value of 387 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 88 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FOO-06-119-01-06-89502.
