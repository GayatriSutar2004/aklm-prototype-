Eligibility And Requirements

Reference FOO-06-119-02-06-90511 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Food South. The reservation workflow supports ordering and records reservation information under the South operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 35 units and a listed service value of 133 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 192 minutes. For ordering, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FOO-06-119-02-06-90511.
