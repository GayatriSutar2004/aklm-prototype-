Eligibility And Requirements

Reference FOO-06-119-08-06-96565 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Food South. The reservation workflow supports ordering and records order information under the South operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 85 units and a listed service value of 293 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 92 minutes. For modification, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FOO-06-119-08-06-96565.
