Support Procedure

Reference FIN-01-185-04-04-52489 governs the support procedure for Atlas Finance Coastal. The transfer workflow supports closure and records transfer information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 45 units and a listed service value of 322 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 200 minutes. For dispute, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-01-185-04-04-52489.
