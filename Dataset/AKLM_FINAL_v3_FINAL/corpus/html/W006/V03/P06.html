Eligibility And Requirements

Reference FIN-01-185-03-06-52106 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Finance Coastal. The statement workflow supports closure and records statement information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 57 units and a listed service value of 360 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 179 minutes. For closure, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-01-185-03-06-52106.
