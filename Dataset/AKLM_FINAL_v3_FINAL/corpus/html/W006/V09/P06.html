Eligibility And Requirements

Reference FIN-01-185-09-06-58160 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Finance Coastal. The statement workflow supports closure and records transfer information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 28 units and a listed service value of 99 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 79 minutes. For closure, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-01-185-09-06-58160.
