Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-07-970-03-06-73657 governs the eligibility and requirements for Pioneer Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports replacement and records inspection information under the Central operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 59 units and a listed service value of 144 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 166 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-07-970-03-06-73657.
