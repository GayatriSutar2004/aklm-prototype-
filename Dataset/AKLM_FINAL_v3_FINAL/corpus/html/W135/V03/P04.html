Support Procedure

Reference AUT-07-970-03-04-73031 governs the support procedure for Pioneer Automotive Central. The inspection workflow supports booking and records vehicle information under the Central operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 65 units and a listed service value of 360 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 83 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-07-970-03-04-73031.
