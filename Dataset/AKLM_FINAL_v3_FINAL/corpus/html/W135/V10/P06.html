Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-07-970-10-06-80720 governs the eligibility and requirements for Pioneer Automotive Central. The vehicle workflow supports inspection and records inspection information under the Central operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 12 units and a listed service value of 50 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 170 minutes. For claim, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-07-970-10-06-80720.
