Support Procedure

Reference AUT-07-970-07-04-77067 governs the support procedure for Pioneer Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports maintenance and records service booking information under the Central operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 72 units and a listed service value of 186 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 137 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-07-970-07-04-77067.
