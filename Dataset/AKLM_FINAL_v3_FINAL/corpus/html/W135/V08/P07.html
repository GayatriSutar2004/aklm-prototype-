Timing And Scheduling

Reference AUT-07-970-08-07-79015 governs the timing and scheduling for Pioneer Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports booking and records part information under the Central operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 45 units and a listed service value of 450 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 94 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-07-970-08-07-79015.
