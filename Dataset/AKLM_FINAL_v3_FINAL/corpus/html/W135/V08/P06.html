Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-07-970-08-06-78702 governs the eligibility and requirements for Pioneer Automotive Central. The part workflow supports booking and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 48 units and a listed service value of 137 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 143 minutes. For claim, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-07-970-08-06-78702.
