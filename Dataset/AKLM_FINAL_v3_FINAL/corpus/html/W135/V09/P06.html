Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-07-970-09-06-79711 governs the eligibility and requirements for Pioneer Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports replacement and records service booking information under the Central operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 30 units and a listed service value of 304 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 66 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-07-970-09-06-79711.
