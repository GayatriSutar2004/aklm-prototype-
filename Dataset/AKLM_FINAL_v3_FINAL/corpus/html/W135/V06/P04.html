Support Procedure

Reference AUT-07-970-06-04-76058 governs the support procedure for Pioneer Automotive Central. The vehicle workflow supports claim and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 90 units and a listed service value of 440 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 33 minutes. For inspection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-07-970-06-04-76058.
