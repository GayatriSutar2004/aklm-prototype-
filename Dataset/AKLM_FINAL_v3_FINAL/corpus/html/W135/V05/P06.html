Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-07-970-05-06-75675 governs the eligibility and requirements for Pioneer Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports claim and records vehicle information under the Central operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 23 units and a listed service value of 57 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 193 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-07-970-05-06-75675.
