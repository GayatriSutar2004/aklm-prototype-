Support Procedure

Reference AUT-07-970-05-04-75049 governs the support procedure for Pioneer Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports inspection and records inspection information under the Central operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 29 units and a listed service value of 273 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 110 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-07-970-05-04-75049.
