Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-07-970-01-06-71639 governs the eligibility and requirements for Pioneer Automotive Central. The inspection workflow supports maintenance and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 16 units and a listed service value of 231 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 139 minutes. For inspection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-07-970-01-06-71639.
