Support Procedure

Reference AUT-07-970-01-04-71013 governs the support procedure for Pioneer Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports maintenance and records warranty information under the Central operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 22 units and a listed service value of 447 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 56 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-07-970-01-04-71013.
