Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-09-419-02-06-81489 governs the eligibility and requirements for Summit Utilities West. The service request workflow supports connection and records connection information under the West operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 28 units and a listed service value of 225 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 117 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-09-419-02-06-81489.
