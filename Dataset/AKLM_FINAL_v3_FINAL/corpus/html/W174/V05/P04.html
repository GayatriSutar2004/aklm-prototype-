Support Procedure

Reference UTI-09-419-05-04-83890 governs the support procedure for Summit Utilities West. The bill workflow supports disconnection and records meter information under the West operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 59 units and a listed service value of 100 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 165 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-09-419-05-04-83890.
