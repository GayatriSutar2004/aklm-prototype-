Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-09-419-04-06-83507 governs the eligibility and requirements for Summit Utilities West. The connection workflow supports disconnection and records service request information under the West operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 71 units and a listed service value of 138 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 144 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-09-419-04-06-83507.
