Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HR-08-750-04-06-40965 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Hr Coastal. The leave request workflow supports approval and records leave request information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 45 units and a listed service value of 315 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 49 minutes. For appeal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HR-08-750-04-06-40965.
