Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HEA-09-086-02-06-10218 governs the eligibility and requirements for Summit Healthcare Central. The care plan workflow supports review and records care plan information under the Central operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 15 units and a listed service value of 103 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 160 minutes. For rescheduling, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-09-086-02-06-10218.
