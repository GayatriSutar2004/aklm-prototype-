Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HEA-09-086-08-06-16272 governs the eligibility and requirements for Summit Healthcare Central. The care plan workflow supports cancellation and records record information under the Central operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 65 units and a listed service value of 263 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 60 minutes. For rescheduling, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-09-086-08-06-16272.
