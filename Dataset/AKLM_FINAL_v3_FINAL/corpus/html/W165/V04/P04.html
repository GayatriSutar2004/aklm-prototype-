Support Procedure

Reference HEA-09-086-04-04-11610 governs the support procedure for Summit Healthcare Central. The care plan workflow supports verification and records referral information under the Central operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 64 units and a listed service value of 232 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 104 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-09-086-04-04-11610.
