Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-07-748-01-06-24125 governs the eligibility and requirements for Pioneer Ecommerce River. The shipment workflow supports delivery and records order information under the River operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 60 units and a listed service value of 290 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 47 minutes. For delivery, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-07-748-01-06-24125.
