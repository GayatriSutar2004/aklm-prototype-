Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-04-744-05-06-00535 governs the eligibility and requirements for Cedar Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports booking and records vehicle information under the Central operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 68 units and a listed service value of 226 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 178 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-04-744-05-06-00535.
