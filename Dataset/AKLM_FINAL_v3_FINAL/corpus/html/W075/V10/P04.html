Support Procedure

Reference AUT-04-744-10-04-04954 governs the support procedure for Cedar Automotive Central. The part workflow supports claim and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 63 units and a listed service value of 435 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 72 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-04-744-10-04-04954.
