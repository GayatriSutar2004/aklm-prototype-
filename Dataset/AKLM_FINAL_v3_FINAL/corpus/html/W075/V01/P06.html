Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-04-744-01-06-96499 governs the eligibility and requirements for Cedar Automotive Central. The inspection workflow supports replacement and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 61 units and a listed service value of 400 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 124 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-04-744-01-06-96499.
