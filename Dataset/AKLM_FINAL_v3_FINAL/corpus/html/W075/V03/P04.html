Support Procedure

Reference AUT-04-744-03-04-97891 governs the support procedure for Cedar Automotive Central. The inspection workflow supports inspection and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 31 units and a listed service value of 108 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 68 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-04-744-03-04-97891.
