Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-04-744-09-06-04571 governs the eligibility and requirements for Cedar Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports claim and records service booking information under the Central operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 75 units and a listed service value of 52 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 51 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-04-744-09-06-04571.
