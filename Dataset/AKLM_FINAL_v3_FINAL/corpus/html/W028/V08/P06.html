Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PRO-02-002-08-06-31369 governs the eligibility and requirements for Nimbus Property Highland. The maintenance request workflow supports renewal and records maintenance request information under the Highland operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 69 units and a listed service value of 277 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 71 minutes. For renewal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PRO-02-002-08-06-31369.
