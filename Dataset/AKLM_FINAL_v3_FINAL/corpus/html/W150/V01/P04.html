Support Procedure

Reference PUB-08-528-01-04-89798 governs the support procedure for Lumen Public Service Lakeside. The permit workflow supports verification and records certificate information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 70 units and a listed service value of 89 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 105 minutes. For appeal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-08-528-01-04-89798.
