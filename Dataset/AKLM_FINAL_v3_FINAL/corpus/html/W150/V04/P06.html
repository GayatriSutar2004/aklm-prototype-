Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-08-528-04-06-93451 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Public Service Lakeside. The request workflow supports renewal and records request information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 89 units and a listed service value of 374 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 138 minutes. For renewal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-08-528-04-06-93451.
