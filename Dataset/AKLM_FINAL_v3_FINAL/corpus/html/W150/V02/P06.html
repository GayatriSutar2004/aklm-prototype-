Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-08-528-02-06-91433 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Public Service Lakeside. The certificate workflow supports review and records application information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 46 units and a listed service value of 40 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 111 minutes. For renewal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-08-528-02-06-91433.
