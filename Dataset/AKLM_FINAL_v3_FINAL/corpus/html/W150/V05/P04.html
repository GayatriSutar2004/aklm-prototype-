Support Procedure

Reference PUB-08-528-05-04-93834 governs the support procedure for Lumen Public Service Lakeside. The appointment workflow supports appeal and records appointment information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 77 units and a listed service value of 336 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 159 minutes. For renewal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-08-528-05-04-93834.
