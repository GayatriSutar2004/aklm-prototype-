Eligibility And Requirements

Reference LOG-06-341-03-06-39034 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Logistics Highland. The parcel workflow supports delivery and records parcel information under the Highland operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 52 units and a listed service value of 241 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 26 minutes. For claim, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference LOG-06-341-03-06-39034.
