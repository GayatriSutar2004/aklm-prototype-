Eligibility And Requirements

Reference LOG-06-341-04-06-40043 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Logistics Highland. The route workflow supports routing and records shipment information under the Highland operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 34 units and a listed service value of 408 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 130 minutes. For routing, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference LOG-06-341-04-06-40043.
