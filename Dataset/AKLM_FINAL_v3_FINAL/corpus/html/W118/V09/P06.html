Eligibility And Requirements

Reference LOG-06-341-09-06-45088 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Logistics Highland. The parcel workflow supports delivery and records delivery information under the Highland operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 23 units and a listed service value of 401 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 107 minutes. For rerouting, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference LOG-06-341-09-06-45088.
