Eligibility And Requirements

Reference LOG-06-341-02-06-38025 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Logistics Highland. The delivery workflow supports claim and records delivery information under the Highland operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 70 units and a listed service value of 74 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 103 minutes. For pickup, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference LOG-06-341-02-06-38025.
