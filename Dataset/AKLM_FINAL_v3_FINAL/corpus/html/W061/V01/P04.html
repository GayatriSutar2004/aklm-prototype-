Support Procedure

Reference EDU-04-226-01-04-85007 governs the support procedure for Cedar Education North. The scholarship workflow supports withdrawal and records scholarship information under the North operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 38 units and a listed service value of 52 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 128 minutes. For submission, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EDU-04-226-01-04-85007.
