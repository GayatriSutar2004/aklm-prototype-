Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-09-456-09-06-96471 governs the eligibility and requirements for Summit Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports booking and records inspection information under the Central operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 79 units and a listed service value of 51 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 76 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-09-456-09-06-96471.
