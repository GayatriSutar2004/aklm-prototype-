Support Procedure

Reference AUT-09-456-01-04-87773 governs the support procedure for Summit Automotive Central. The inspection workflow supports inspection and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 71 units and a listed service value of 194 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 66 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-09-456-01-04-87773.
