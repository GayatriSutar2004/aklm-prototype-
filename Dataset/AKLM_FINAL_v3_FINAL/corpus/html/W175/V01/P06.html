Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-09-456-01-06-88399 governs the eligibility and requirements for Summit Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports claim and records warranty information under the Central operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 65 units and a listed service value of 399 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 149 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-09-456-01-06-88399.
