Support Procedure

Reference AUT-09-456-08-04-94836 governs the support procedure for Summit Automotive Central. The part workflow supports claim and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 24 units and a listed service value of 100 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 70 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-09-456-08-04-94836.
