Support Procedure

Reference AUT-09-456-02-04-88782 governs the support procedure for Summit Automotive Central. The part workflow supports claim and records vehicle information under the Central operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 53 units and a listed service value of 361 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 170 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-09-456-02-04-88782.
