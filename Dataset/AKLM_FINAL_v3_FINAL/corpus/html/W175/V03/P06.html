Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-09-456-03-06-90417 governs the eligibility and requirements for Summit Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports maintenance and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 29 units and a listed service value of 312 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 176 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-09-456-03-06-90417.
