Support Procedure

Reference AUT-09-456-03-04-89791 governs the support procedure for Summit Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports maintenance and records warranty information under the Central operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 35 units and a listed service value of 107 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 93 minutes. For claim, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-09-456-03-04-89791.
