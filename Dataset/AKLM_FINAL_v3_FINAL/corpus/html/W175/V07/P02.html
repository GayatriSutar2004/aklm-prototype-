Pricing And Commercial Terms

Reference AUT-09-456-07-02-93201 governs the pricing and commercial terms for Summit Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports inspection and records vehicle information under the Central operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 48 units and a listed service value of 149 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 64 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-09-456-07-02-93201.
