Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-09-456-07-06-94453 governs the eligibility and requirements for Summit Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports claim and records vehicle information under the Central operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 36 units and a listed service value of 138 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 49 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-09-456-07-06-94453.
