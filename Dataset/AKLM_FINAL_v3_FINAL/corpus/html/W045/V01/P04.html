Support Procedure

Reference HEA-03-631-01-04-58303 governs the support procedure for Vertex Healthcare Central. The membership workflow supports rescheduling and records referral information under the Central operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 50 units and a listed service value of 69 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 124 minutes. For rescheduling, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-03-631-01-04-58303.
