Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HEA-03-631-08-06-65992 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Healthcare Central. The care plan workflow supports review and records session information under the Central operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 76 units and a listed service value of 180 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 30 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-03-631-08-06-65992.
