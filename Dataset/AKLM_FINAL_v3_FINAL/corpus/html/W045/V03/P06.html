Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HEA-03-631-03-06-60947 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Healthcare Central. The referral workflow supports cancellation and records referral information under the Central operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 87 units and a listed service value of 187 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 53 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-03-631-03-06-60947.
