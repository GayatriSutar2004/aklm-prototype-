Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HEA-03-631-10-06-68010 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Healthcare Central. The appointment workflow supports verification and records referral information under the Central operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 40 units and a listed service value of 93 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 57 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-03-631-10-06-68010.
