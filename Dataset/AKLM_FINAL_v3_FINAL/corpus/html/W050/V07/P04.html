Support Procedure

Reference PUB-03-816-07-04-03952 governs the support procedure for Vertex Public Service Lakeside. The appointment workflow supports appeal and records appointment information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 37 units and a listed service value of 250 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 161 minutes. For renewal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-03-816-07-04-03952.
