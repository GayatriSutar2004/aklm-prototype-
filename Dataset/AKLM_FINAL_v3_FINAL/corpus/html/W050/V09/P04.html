Support Procedure

Reference PUB-03-816-09-04-05970 governs the support procedure for Vertex Public Service Lakeside. The permit workflow supports submission and records application information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 80 units and a listed service value of 163 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 188 minutes. For renewal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-03-816-09-04-05970.
