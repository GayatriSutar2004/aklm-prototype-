Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-03-816-05-06-02560 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Public Service Lakeside. The appointment workflow supports renewal and records appointment information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 67 units and a listed service value of 121 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 36 minutes. For verification, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-03-816-05-06-02560.
