Support Procedure

Reference PUB-03-816-01-04-97898 governs the support procedure for Vertex Public Service Lakeside. The appointment workflow supports appeal and records permit information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 66 units and a listed service value of 90 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 80 minutes. For appeal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-03-816-01-04-97898.
