Support Procedure

Reference PUB-03-816-04-04-00925 governs the support procedure for Vertex Public Service Lakeside. The case workflow supports submission and records request information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 12 units and a listed service value of 170 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 30 minutes. For submission, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-03-816-04-04-00925.
