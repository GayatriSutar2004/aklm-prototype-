Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-03-816-04-06-01551 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Public Service Lakeside. The certificate workflow supports review and records application information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 85 units and a listed service value of 375 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 113 minutes. For renewal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-03-816-04-06-01551.
