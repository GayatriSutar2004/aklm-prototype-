Support Procedure

Reference PUB-03-816-03-04-99916 governs the support procedure for Vertex Public Service Lakeside. The permit workflow supports submission and records appointment information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 30 units and a listed service value of 424 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 107 minutes. For appeal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-03-816-03-04-99916.
