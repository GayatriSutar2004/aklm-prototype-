Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-03-816-06-06-03569 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Public Service Lakeside. The request workflow supports appeal and records permit information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 49 units and a listed service value of 288 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 140 minutes. For renewal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-03-816-06-06-03569.
