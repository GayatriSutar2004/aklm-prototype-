Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TEL-06-156-05-06-01457 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Telecom East. The number workflow supports transfer and records device information under the East operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 79 units and a listed service value of 133 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 97 minutes. For suspension, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TEL-06-156-05-06-01457.
