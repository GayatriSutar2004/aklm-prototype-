Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TEL-06-156-01-06-97421 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Telecom East. The SIM workflow supports replacement and records SIM information under the East operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 72 units and a listed service value of 307 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 43 minutes. For suspension, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TEL-06-156-01-06-97421.
