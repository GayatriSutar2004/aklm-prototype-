Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TEL-06-156-03-06-99439 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Telecom East. The support request workflow supports upgrade and records number information under the East operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 36 units and a listed service value of 220 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 70 minutes. For suspension, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TEL-06-156-03-06-99439.
