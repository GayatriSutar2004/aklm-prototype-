Eligibility And Requirements

Reference RES-02-335-04-06-98604 governs the eligibility and requirements for Nimbus Research Metro. The submission workflow supports access and records study information under the Metro operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 75 units and a listed service value of 152 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 155 minutes. For revision, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference RES-02-335-04-06-98604.
