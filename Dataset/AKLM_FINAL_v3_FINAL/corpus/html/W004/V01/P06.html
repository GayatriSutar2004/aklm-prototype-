Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HOS-01-111-01-06-34250 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Hospitality West. The guest workflow supports cancellation and records service information under the West operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 55 units and a listed service value of 186 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 61 minutes. For upgrade, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HOS-01-111-01-06-34250.
