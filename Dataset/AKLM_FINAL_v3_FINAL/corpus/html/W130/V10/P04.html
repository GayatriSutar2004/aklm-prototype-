Support Procedure

Reference PUB-07-785-10-04-40499 governs the support procedure for Pioneer Public Service Lakeside. The request workflow supports appeal and records application information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 81 units and a listed service value of 245 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 131 minutes. For renewal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-07-785-10-04-40499.
