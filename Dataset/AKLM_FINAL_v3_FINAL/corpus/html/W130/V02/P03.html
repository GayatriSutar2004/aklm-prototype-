Operating Policy

Reference PUB-07-785-02-03-32114 governs the operating policy for Pioneer Public Service Lakeside. The application workflow supports review and records application information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 70 units and a listed service value of 280 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 72 minutes. For renewal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-07-785-02-03-32114.
