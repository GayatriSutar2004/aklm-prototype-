Support Procedure

Reference PUB-07-785-04-04-34445 governs the support procedure for Pioneer Public Service Lakeside. The request workflow supports appeal and records appointment information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 31 units and a listed service value of 85 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 50 minutes. For appeal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-07-785-04-04-34445.
