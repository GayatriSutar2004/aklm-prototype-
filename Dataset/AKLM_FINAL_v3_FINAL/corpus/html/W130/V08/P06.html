Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-07-785-08-06-39107 governs the eligibility and requirements for Pioneer Public Service Lakeside. The request workflow supports renewal and records application information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 32 units and a listed service value of 116 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 187 minutes. For verification, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-07-785-08-06-39107.
