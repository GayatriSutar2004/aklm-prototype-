Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-07-785-05-06-36080 governs the eligibility and requirements for Pioneer Public Service Lakeside. The application workflow supports verification and records certificate information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 86 units and a listed service value of 457 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 56 minutes. For renewal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-07-785-05-06-36080.
