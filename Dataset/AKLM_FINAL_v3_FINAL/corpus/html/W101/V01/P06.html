Eligibility And Requirements

Reference EDU-06-709-01-06-02393 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Education North. The module workflow supports attendance and records assessment information under the North operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 81 units and a listed service value of 425 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 40 minutes. For attendance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EDU-06-709-01-06-02393.
