Eligibility And Requirements

Reference EDU-06-709-08-06-09456 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Education North. The assessment workflow supports withdrawal and records assessment information under the North operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 34 units and a listed service value of 331 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 44 minutes. For enrolment, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EDU-06-709-08-06-09456.
