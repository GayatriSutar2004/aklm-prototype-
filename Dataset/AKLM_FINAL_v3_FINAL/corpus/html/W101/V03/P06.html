Eligibility And Requirements

Reference EDU-06-709-03-06-04411 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Education North. The scholarship workflow supports enrolment and records course information under the North operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 45 units and a listed service value of 338 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 67 minutes. For attendance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EDU-06-709-03-06-04411.
