Support Procedure

Reference EDU-06-709-04-04-04794 governs the support procedure for Harbor Education North. The assessment workflow supports enrolment and records certificate information under the North operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 33 units and a listed service value of 300 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 88 minutes. For attendance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EDU-06-709-04-04-04794.
