Eligibility And Requirements

Reference EDU-06-709-02-06-03402 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Education North. The assessment workflow supports withdrawal and records lab information under the North operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 63 units and a listed service value of 171 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 144 minutes. For enrolment, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EDU-06-709-02-06-03402.
