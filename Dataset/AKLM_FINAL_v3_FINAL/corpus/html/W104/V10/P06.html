Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HOS-06-820-10-06-35231 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Hospitality West. The reservation workflow supports amenity access and records room information under the West operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 55 units and a listed service value of 425 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 117 minutes. For amenity access, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HOS-06-820-10-06-35231.
