Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HOS-06-820-04-06-29177 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Hospitality West. The reservation workflow supports amenity access and records reservation information under the West operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 84 units and a listed service value of 265 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 36 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HOS-06-820-04-06-29177.
