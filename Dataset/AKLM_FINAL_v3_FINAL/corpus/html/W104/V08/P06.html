Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HOS-06-820-08-06-33213 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Hospitality West. The service workflow supports check-in and records guest information under the West operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 12 units and a listed service value of 91 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 90 minutes. For amenity access, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HOS-06-820-08-06-33213.
