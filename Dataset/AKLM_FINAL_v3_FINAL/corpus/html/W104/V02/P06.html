Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HOS-06-820-02-06-27159 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Hospitality West. The service workflow supports check-in and records package information under the West operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 41 units and a listed service value of 352 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 190 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HOS-06-820-02-06-27159.
