Support Procedure

Reference TEL-10-125-01-04-30315 governs the support procedure for Aurora Telecom East. The support request workflow supports upgrade and records support request information under the East operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 18 units and a listed service value of 438 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 161 minutes. For upgrade, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TEL-10-125-01-04-30315.
