Support Procedure

Reference FIN-05-154-01-04-82982 governs the support procedure for Solstice Finance Coastal. The statement workflow supports closure and records transfer information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 39 units and a listed service value of 157 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 89 minutes. For dispute, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-05-154-01-04-82982.
