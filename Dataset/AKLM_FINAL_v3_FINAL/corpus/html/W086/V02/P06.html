Eligibility And Requirements

Reference FIN-05-154-02-06-84617 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Finance Coastal. The wallet workflow supports dispute and records wallet information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 15 units and a listed service value of 108 units. For dispute, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-05-154-02-06-84617.
