Support Procedure

Reference FIN-05-154-03-04-85000 governs the support procedure for Solstice Finance Coastal. The payment workflow supports dispute and records payment information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 82 units and a listed service value of 70 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 116 minutes. For dispute, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-05-154-03-04-85000.
