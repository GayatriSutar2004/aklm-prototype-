Eligibility And Requirements

Reference FIN-05-154-10-06-92689 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Finance Coastal. The transfer workflow supports verification and records transfer information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 29 units and a listed service value of 181 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 22 minutes. For closure, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-05-154-10-06-92689.
