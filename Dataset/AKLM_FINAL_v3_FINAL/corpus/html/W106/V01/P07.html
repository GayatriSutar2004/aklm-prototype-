Timing And Scheduling

Reference FIN-06-894-01-07-42301 governs the timing and scheduling for Harbor Finance Coastal. The beneficiary workflow supports closure and records account information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 15 units and a listed service value of 338 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 128 minutes. For dispute, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-06-894-01-07-42301.
