Eligibility And Requirements

Reference FIN-06-894-01-06-41988 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Finance Coastal. The statement workflow supports reconciliation and records statement information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 18 units and a listed service value of 446 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 177 minutes. For closure, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-06-894-01-06-41988.
