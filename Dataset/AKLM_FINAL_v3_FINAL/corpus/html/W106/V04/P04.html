Support Procedure

Reference FIN-06-894-04-04-44389 governs the support procedure for Harbor Finance Coastal. The beneficiary workflow supports dispute and records payment information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 49 units and a listed service value of 321 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 44 minutes. For closure, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-06-894-04-04-44389.
