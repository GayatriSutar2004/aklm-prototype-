Eligibility And Requirements

Reference FIN-06-894-04-06-45015 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Finance Coastal. The wallet workflow supports transfer and records transfer information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 43 units and a listed service value of 105 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 127 minutes. For transfer, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-06-894-04-06-45015.
