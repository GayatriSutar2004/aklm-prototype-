Security And Access

Reference FIN-06-894-04-08-45641 governs the security and access for Harbor Finance Coastal. The transfer workflow supports verification and records payment information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 37 units and a listed service value of 310 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 29 minutes. For reconciliation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-06-894-04-08-45641.
