Support Procedure

Reference FIN-06-894-09-04-49434 governs the support procedure for Harbor Finance Coastal. The statement workflow supports dispute and records transfer information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 38 units and a listed service value of 314 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 21 minutes. For transfer, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-06-894-09-04-49434.
