Eligibility And Requirements

Reference FIN-06-894-09-06-50060 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Finance Coastal. The payment workflow supports dispute and records payment information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 32 units and a listed service value of 98 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 104 minutes. For reconciliation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-06-894-09-06-50060.
