Support Procedure

Reference FIN-06-894-02-04-42371 governs the support procedure for Harbor Finance Coastal. The transfer workflow supports closure and records transfer information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 85 units and a listed service value of 408 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 198 minutes. For dispute, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-06-894-02-04-42371.
