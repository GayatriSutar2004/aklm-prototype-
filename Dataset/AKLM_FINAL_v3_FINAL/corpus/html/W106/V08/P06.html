Eligibility And Requirements

Reference FIN-06-894-08-06-49051 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Finance Coastal. The beneficiary workflow supports closure and records statement information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 50 units and a listed service value of 352 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 181 minutes. For transfer, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-06-894-08-06-49051.
