Support Procedure

Reference FIN-06-894-08-04-48425 governs the support procedure for Harbor Finance Coastal. The transfer workflow supports closure and records wallet information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 56 units and a listed service value of 147 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 98 minutes. For closure, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-06-894-08-04-48425.
