Eligibility And Requirements

Reference FIN-06-894-06-06-47033 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Finance Coastal. The transfer workflow supports reconciliation and records wallet information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 86 units and a listed service value of 439 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 154 minutes. For transfer, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-06-894-06-06-47033.
