Support Procedure

Reference FIN-06-894-10-04-50443 governs the support procedure for Harbor Finance Coastal. The beneficiary workflow supports transfer and records beneficiary information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 20 units and a listed service value of 60 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 125 minutes. For closure, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-06-894-10-04-50443.
