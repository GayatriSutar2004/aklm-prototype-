Eligibility And Requirements

Reference FIN-06-894-10-06-51069 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Finance Coastal. The wallet workflow supports transfer and records wallet information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 14 units and a listed service value of 265 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 27 minutes. For transfer, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-06-894-10-06-51069.
