Eligibility And Requirements

Reference FIN-06-894-05-06-46024 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Finance Coastal. The account workflow supports verification and records beneficiary information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 25 units and a listed service value of 272 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 50 minutes. For closure, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-06-894-05-06-46024.
