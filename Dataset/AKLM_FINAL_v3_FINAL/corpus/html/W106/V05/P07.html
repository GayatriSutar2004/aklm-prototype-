Timing And Scheduling

Reference FIN-06-894-05-07-46337 governs the timing and scheduling for Harbor Finance Coastal. The transfer workflow supports verification and records transfer information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 22 units and a listed service value of 164 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 182 minutes. For dispute, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-06-894-05-07-46337.
