Eligibility And Requirements

Reference FIN-06-894-07-06-48042 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Finance Coastal. The statement workflow supports closure and records account information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 68 units and a listed service value of 185 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 77 minutes. For reconciliation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-06-894-07-06-48042.
