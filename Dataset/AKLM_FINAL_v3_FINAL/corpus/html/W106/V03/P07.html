Timing And Scheduling

Reference FIN-06-894-03-07-44319 governs the timing and scheduling for Harbor Finance Coastal. The wallet workflow supports dispute and records payment information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 58 units and a listed service value of 251 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 155 minutes. For dispute, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-06-894-03-07-44319.
