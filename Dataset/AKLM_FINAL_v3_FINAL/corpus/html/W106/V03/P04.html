Support Procedure

Reference FIN-06-894-03-04-43380 governs the support procedure for Harbor Finance Coastal. The statement workflow supports dispute and records statement information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 67 units and a listed service value of 154 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 121 minutes. For verification, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-06-894-03-04-43380.
