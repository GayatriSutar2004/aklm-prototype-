Eligibility And Requirements

Reference INS-06-931-03-06-51925 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Insurance Metro. The document workflow supports appeal and records renewal information under the Metro operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 80 units and a listed service value of 279 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 159 minutes. For renewal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference INS-06-931-03-06-51925.
