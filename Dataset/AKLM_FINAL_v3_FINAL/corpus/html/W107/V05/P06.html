Eligibility And Requirements

Reference INS-06-931-05-06-53943 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Insurance Metro. The renewal workflow supports verification and records document information under the Metro operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 44 units and a listed service value of 192 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 186 minutes. For renewal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference INS-06-931-05-06-53943.
