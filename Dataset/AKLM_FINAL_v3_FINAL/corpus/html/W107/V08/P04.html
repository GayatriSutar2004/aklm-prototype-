Support Procedure

Reference INS-06-931-08-04-56344 governs the support procedure for Harbor Insurance Metro. The claim workflow supports assessment and records renewal information under the Metro operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 75 units and a listed service value of 67 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 53 minutes. For renewal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference INS-06-931-08-04-56344.
