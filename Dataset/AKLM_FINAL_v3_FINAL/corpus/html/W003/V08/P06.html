Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-01-074-08-06-33394 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Travel East. The boarding pass workflow supports booking and records route information under the East operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 68 units and a listed service value of 172 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 110 minutes. For modification, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-01-074-08-06-33394.
