Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-01-074-10-06-35412 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Travel East. The reservation workflow supports modification and records transfer information under the East operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 32 units and a listed service value of 85 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 137 minutes. For transfer, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-01-074-10-06-35412.
