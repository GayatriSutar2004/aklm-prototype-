Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-01-074-06-06-31376 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Travel East. The luggage workflow supports check-in and records luggage information under the East operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 25 units and a listed service value of 259 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 83 minutes. For modification, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-01-074-06-06-31376.
