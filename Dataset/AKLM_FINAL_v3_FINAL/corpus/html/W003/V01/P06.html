Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-01-074-01-06-26331 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Travel East. The transfer workflow supports cancellation and records route information under the East operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 36 units and a listed service value of 266 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 106 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-01-074-01-06-26331.
