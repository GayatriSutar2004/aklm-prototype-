Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-01-074-03-06-28349 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Travel East. The route workflow supports transfer and records transfer information under the East operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 79 units and a listed service value of 179 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 133 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-01-074-03-06-28349.
