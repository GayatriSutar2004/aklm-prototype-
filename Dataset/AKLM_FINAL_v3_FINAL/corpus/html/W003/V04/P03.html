Operating Policy

Reference TRA-01-074-04-03-28419 governs the operating policy for Atlas Travel East. The transfer workflow supports transfer and records transfer information under the East operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 70 units and a listed service value of 249 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 22 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-01-074-04-03-28419.
