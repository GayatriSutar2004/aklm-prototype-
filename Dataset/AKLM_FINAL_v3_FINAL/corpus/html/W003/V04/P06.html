Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-01-074-04-06-29358 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Travel East. The reservation workflow supports transfer and records boarding pass information under the East operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 61 units and a listed service value of 346 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 56 minutes. For modification, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-01-074-04-06-29358.
