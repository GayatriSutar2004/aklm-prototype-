Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-01-074-02-06-27340 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Travel East. The boarding pass workflow supports booking and records itinerary information under the East operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 18 units and a listed service value of 433 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 29 minutes. For modification, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-01-074-02-06-27340.
