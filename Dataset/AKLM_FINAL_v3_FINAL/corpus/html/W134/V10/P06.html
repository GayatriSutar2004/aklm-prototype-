Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-07-933-10-06-72801 governs the eligibility and requirements for Pioneer Utilities West. The service request workflow supports billing and records connection information under the West operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 72 units and a listed service value of 130 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 34 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-07-933-10-06-72801.
