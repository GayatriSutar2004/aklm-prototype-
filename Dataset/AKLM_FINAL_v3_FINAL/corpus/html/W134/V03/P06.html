Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-07-933-03-06-65738 governs the eligibility and requirements for Pioneer Utilities West. The meter workflow supports disconnection and records connection information under the West operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 40 units and a listed service value of 224 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 30 minutes. For reconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-07-933-03-06-65738.
