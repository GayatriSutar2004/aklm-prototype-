Support Procedure

Reference UTI-07-933-08-04-70157 governs the support procedure for Pioneer Utilities West. The connection workflow supports reconnection and records connection information under the West operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 35 units and a listed service value of 433 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 105 minutes. For reconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-07-933-08-04-70157.
