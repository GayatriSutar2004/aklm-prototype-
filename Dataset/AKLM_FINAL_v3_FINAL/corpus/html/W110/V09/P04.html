Support Procedure

Reference PUB-06-045-09-04-81110 governs the support procedure for Harbor Public Service Lakeside. The permit workflow supports appeal and records application information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 35 units and a listed service value of 415 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 22 minutes. For appeal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-06-045-09-04-81110.
