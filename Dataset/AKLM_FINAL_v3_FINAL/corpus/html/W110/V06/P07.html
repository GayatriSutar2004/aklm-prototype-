Timing And Scheduling

Reference PUB-06-045-06-07-79022 governs the timing and scheduling for Harbor Public Service Lakeside. The permit workflow supports review and records request information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 80 units and a listed service value of 432 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 106 minutes. For appeal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-06-045-06-07-79022.
