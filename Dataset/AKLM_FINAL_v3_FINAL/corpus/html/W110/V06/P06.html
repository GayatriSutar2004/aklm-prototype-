Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-06-045-06-06-78709 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Public Service Lakeside. The request workflow supports review and records permit information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 83 units and a listed service value of 119 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 155 minutes. For renewal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-06-045-06-06-78709.
