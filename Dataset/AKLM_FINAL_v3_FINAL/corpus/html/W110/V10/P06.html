Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-06-045-10-06-82745 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Public Service Lakeside. The certificate workflow supports verification and records case information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 90 units and a listed service value of 366 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 28 minutes. For renewal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-06-045-10-06-82745.
