Support Procedure

Reference PUB-06-045-10-04-82119 governs the support procedure for Harbor Public Service Lakeside. The case workflow supports appeal and records appointment information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 17 units and a listed service value of 161 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 126 minutes. For submission, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-06-045-10-04-82119.
