Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-06-045-01-06-73664 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Public Service Lakeside. The permit workflow supports review and records appointment information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 15 units and a listed service value of 126 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 178 minutes. For review, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-06-045-01-06-73664.
