Pricing And Commercial Terms

Reference PUB-06-045-01-02-72412 governs the pricing and commercial terms for Harbor Public Service Lakeside. The application workflow supports submission and records appointment information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 27 units and a listed service value of 137 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 193 minutes. For renewal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-06-045-01-02-72412.
