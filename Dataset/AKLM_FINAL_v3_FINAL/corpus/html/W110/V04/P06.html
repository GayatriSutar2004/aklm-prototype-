Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-06-045-04-06-76691 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Public Service Lakeside. The certificate workflow supports verification and records certificate information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 40 units and a listed service value of 206 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 128 minutes. For appeal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-06-045-04-06-76691.
