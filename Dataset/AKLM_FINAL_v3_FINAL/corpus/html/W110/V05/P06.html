Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-06-045-05-06-77700 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Public Service Lakeside. The appointment workflow supports submission and records appointment information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 22 units and a listed service value of 373 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 51 minutes. For submission, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-06-045-05-06-77700.
