Support Procedure

Reference PUB-06-045-05-04-77074 governs the support procedure for Harbor Public Service Lakeside. The application workflow supports verification and records application information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 28 units and a listed service value of 168 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 149 minutes. For appeal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-06-045-05-04-77074.
