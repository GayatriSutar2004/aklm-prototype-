Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-06-045-08-06-80727 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Public Service Lakeside. The case workflow supports renewal and records appointment information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 47 units and a listed service value of 453 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 182 minutes. For renewal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-06-045-08-06-80727.
