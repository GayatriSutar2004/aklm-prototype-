Support Procedure

Reference PUB-06-045-08-04-80101 governs the support procedure for Harbor Public Service Lakeside. The request workflow supports renewal and records case information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 53 units and a listed service value of 248 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 99 minutes. For submission, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-06-045-08-04-80101.
