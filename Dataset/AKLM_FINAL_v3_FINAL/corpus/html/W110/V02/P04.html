Support Procedure

Reference PUB-06-045-02-04-74047 governs the support procedure for Harbor Public Service Lakeside. The request workflow supports renewal and records application information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 82 units and a listed service value of 88 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 199 minutes. For review, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-06-045-02-04-74047.
