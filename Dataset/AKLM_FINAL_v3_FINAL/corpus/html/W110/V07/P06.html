Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-06-045-07-06-79718 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Public Service Lakeside. The permit workflow supports review and records application information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 65 units and a listed service value of 286 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 78 minutes. For submission, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-06-045-07-06-79718.
