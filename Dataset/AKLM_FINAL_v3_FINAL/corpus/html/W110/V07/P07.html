Timing And Scheduling

Reference PUB-06-045-07-07-80031 governs the timing and scheduling for Harbor Public Service Lakeside. The case workflow supports renewal and records permit information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 62 units and a listed service value of 178 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 29 minutes. For review, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-06-045-07-07-80031.
