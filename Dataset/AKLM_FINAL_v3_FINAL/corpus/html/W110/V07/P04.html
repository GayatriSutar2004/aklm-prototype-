Support Procedure

Reference PUB-06-045-07-04-79092 governs the support procedure for Harbor Public Service Lakeside. The appointment workflow supports review and records request information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 71 units and a listed service value of 81 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 176 minutes. For appeal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-06-045-07-04-79092.
