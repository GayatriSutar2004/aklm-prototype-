Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-06-045-03-06-75682 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Public Service Lakeside. The application workflow supports appeal and records case information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 58 units and a listed service value of 39 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 24 minutes. For submission, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-06-045-03-06-75682.
