Support Procedure

Reference PUB-06-045-03-04-75056 governs the support procedure for Harbor Public Service Lakeside. The permit workflow supports renewal and records appointment information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 64 units and a listed service value of 255 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 122 minutes. For appeal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-06-045-03-04-75056.
