Pricing And Commercial Terms

Reference PUB-06-045-03-02-74430 governs the pricing and commercial terms for Harbor Public Service Lakeside. The appointment workflow supports renewal and records case information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 70 units and a listed service value of 50 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 39 minutes. For renewal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-06-045-03-02-74430.
