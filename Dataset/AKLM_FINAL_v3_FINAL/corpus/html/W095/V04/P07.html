Timing And Scheduling

Reference AUT-05-487-04-07-58219 governs the timing and scheduling for Solstice Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports claim and records service booking information under the Central operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 68 units and a listed service value of 456 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 30 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-05-487-04-07-58219.
