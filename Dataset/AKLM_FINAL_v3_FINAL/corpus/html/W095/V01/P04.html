Support Procedure

Reference AUT-05-487-01-04-54253 governs the support procedure for Solstice Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports booking and records vehicle information under the Central operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 52 units and a listed service value of 279 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 46 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-05-487-01-04-54253.
