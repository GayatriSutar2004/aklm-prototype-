Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-05-487-01-06-54879 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports replacement and records inspection information under the Central operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 46 units and a listed service value of 63 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 129 minutes. For claim, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-05-487-01-06-54879.
