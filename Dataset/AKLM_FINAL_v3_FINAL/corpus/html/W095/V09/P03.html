Operating Policy

Reference AUT-05-487-09-03-62012 governs the operating policy for Solstice Automotive Central. The vehicle workflow supports inspection and records inspection information under the Central operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 69 units and a listed service value of 39 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 22 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-05-487-09-03-62012.
