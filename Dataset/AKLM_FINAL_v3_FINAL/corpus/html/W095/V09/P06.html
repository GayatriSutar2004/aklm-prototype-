Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-05-487-09-06-62951 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Automotive Central. The inspection workflow supports inspection and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 60 units and a listed service value of 136 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 56 minutes. For inspection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-05-487-09-06-62951.
