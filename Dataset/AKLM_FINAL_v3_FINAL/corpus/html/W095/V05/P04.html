Support Procedure

Reference AUT-05-487-05-04-58289 governs the support procedure for Solstice Automotive Central. The inspection workflow supports claim and records service booking information under the Central operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 59 units and a listed service value of 105 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 100 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-05-487-05-04-58289.
