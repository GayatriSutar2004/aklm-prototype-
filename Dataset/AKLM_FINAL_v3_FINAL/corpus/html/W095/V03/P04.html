Support Procedure

Reference AUT-05-487-03-04-56271 governs the support procedure for Solstice Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports inspection and records inspection information under the Central operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 16 units and a listed service value of 192 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 73 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-05-487-03-04-56271.
