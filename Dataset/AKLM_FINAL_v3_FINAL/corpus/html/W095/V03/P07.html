Timing And Scheduling

Reference AUT-05-487-03-07-57210 governs the timing and scheduling for Solstice Automotive Central. The part workflow supports claim and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 86 units and a listed service value of 289 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 107 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-05-487-03-07-57210.
