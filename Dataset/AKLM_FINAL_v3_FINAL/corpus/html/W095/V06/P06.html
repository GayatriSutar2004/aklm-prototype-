Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-05-487-06-06-59924 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Automotive Central. The vehicle workflow supports booking and records vehicle information under the Central operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 35 units and a listed service value of 56 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 106 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-05-487-06-06-59924.
