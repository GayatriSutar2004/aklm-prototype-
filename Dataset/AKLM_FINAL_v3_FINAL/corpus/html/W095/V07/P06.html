Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-05-487-07-06-60933 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports replacement and records service booking information under the Central operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 17 units and a listed service value of 223 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 29 minutes. For inspection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-05-487-07-06-60933.
