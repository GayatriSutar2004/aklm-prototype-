Support Procedure

Reference ECO-03-779-04-04-93006 governs the support procedure for Vertex Ecommerce River. The order workflow supports delivery and records shipment information under the River operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 72 units and a listed service value of 250 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 75 minutes. For return, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-03-779-04-04-93006.
