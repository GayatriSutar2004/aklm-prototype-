Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-03-779-07-06-96659 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Ecommerce River. The invoice workflow supports purchase and records invoice information under the River operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 12 units and a listed service value of 114 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 108 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-03-779-07-06-96659.
