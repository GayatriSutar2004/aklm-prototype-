Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-03-779-09-06-98677 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Ecommerce River. The product workflow supports delivery and records shipment information under the River operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 55 units and a listed service value of 448 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 135 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-03-779-09-06-98677.
