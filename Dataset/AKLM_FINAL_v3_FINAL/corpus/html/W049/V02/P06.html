Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-03-779-02-06-91614 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Ecommerce River. The order workflow supports cancellation and records shipment information under the River operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 23 units and a listed service value of 121 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 131 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-03-779-02-06-91614.
