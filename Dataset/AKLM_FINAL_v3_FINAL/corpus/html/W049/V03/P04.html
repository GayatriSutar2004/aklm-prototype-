Support Procedure

Reference ECO-03-779-03-04-91997 governs the support procedure for Vertex Ecommerce River. The invoice workflow supports cancellation and records return information under the River operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 90 units and a listed service value of 83 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 152 minutes. For delivery, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-03-779-03-04-91997.
