Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-09-234-01-06-40885 governs the eligibility and requirements for Summit Ecommerce River. The invoice workflow supports purchase and records return information under the River operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 30 units and a listed service value of 37 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 57 minutes. For purchase, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-09-234-01-06-40885.
