Support Procedure

Reference FIN-09-123-02-04-17511 governs the support procedure for Summit Finance Coastal. The transfer workflow supports verification and records transfer information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 40 units and a listed service value of 239 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 32 minutes. For dispute, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-09-123-02-04-17511.
