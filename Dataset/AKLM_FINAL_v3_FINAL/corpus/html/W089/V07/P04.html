Support Procedure

Reference ECO-05-265-07-04-12793 governs the support procedure for Solstice Ecommerce River. The invoice workflow supports replacement and records invoice information under the River operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 67 units and a listed service value of 77 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 35 minutes. For purchase, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-05-265-07-04-12793.
