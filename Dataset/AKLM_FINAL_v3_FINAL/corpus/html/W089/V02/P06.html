Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-05-265-02-06-08374 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Ecommerce River. The return workflow supports return and records invoice information under the River operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 72 units and a listed service value of 289 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 141 minutes. For purchase, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-05-265-02-06-08374.
