Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-05-265-06-06-12410 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Ecommerce River. The order workflow supports replacement and records order information under the River operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 79 units and a listed service value of 115 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 195 minutes. For purchase, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-05-265-06-06-12410.
