Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-05-265-04-06-10392 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Ecommerce River. The cart workflow supports cancellation and records shipment information under the River operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 36 units and a listed service value of 202 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 168 minutes. For purchase, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-05-265-04-06-10392.
