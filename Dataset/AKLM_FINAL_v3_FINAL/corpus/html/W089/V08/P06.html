Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-05-265-08-06-14428 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Ecommerce River. The return workflow supports return and records cart information under the River operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 43 units and a listed service value of 449 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 41 minutes. For return, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-05-265-08-06-14428.
