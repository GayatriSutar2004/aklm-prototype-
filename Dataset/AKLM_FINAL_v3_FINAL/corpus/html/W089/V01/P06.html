Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-05-265-01-06-07365 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Ecommerce River. The product workflow supports replacement and records shipment information under the River operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 90 units and a listed service value of 122 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 37 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-05-265-01-06-07365.
