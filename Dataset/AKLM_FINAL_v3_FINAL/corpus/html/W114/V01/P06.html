Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-06-193-01-06-05340 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Utilities West. The meter workflow supports reconnection and records service request information under the West operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 12 units and a listed service value of 227 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 179 minutes. For inspection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-06-193-01-06-05340.
