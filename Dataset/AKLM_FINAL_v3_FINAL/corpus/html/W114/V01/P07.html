Timing And Scheduling

Reference UTI-06-193-01-07-05653 governs the timing and scheduling for Harbor Utilities West. The service request workflow supports reconnection and records meter information under the West operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 88 units and a listed service value of 119 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 130 minutes. For connection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-06-193-01-07-05653.
