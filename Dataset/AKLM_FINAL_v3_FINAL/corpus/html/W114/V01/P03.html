Operating Policy

Reference UTI-06-193-01-03-04401 governs the operating policy for Harbor Utilities West. The connection workflow supports connection and records meter information under the West operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 21 units and a listed service value of 130 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 145 minutes. For reconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-06-193-01-03-04401.
