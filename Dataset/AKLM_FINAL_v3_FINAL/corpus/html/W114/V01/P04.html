Support Procedure

Reference UTI-06-193-01-04-04714 governs the support procedure for Harbor Utilities West. The account workflow supports reconnection and records account information under the West operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 18 units and a listed service value of 443 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 96 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-06-193-01-04-04714.
