Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-06-193-10-06-14421 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Utilities West. The connection workflow supports inspection and records inspection information under the West operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 87 units and a listed service value of 46 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 29 minutes. For connection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-06-193-10-06-14421.
