Pricing And Commercial Terms

Reference UTI-06-193-10-02-13169 governs the pricing and commercial terms for Harbor Utilities West. The inspection workflow supports billing and records inspection information under the West operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 20 units and a listed service value of 57 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 44 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-06-193-10-02-13169.
