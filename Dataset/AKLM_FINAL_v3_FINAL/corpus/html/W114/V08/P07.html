Timing And Scheduling

Reference UTI-06-193-08-07-12716 governs the timing and scheduling for Harbor Utilities West. The bill workflow supports disconnection and records meter information under the West operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 41 units and a listed service value of 446 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 134 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-06-193-08-07-12716.
