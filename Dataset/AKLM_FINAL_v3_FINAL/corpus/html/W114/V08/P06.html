Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-06-193-08-06-12403 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Utilities West. The service request workflow supports disconnection and records service request information under the West operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 44 units and a listed service value of 133 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 183 minutes. For reconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-06-193-08-06-12403.
