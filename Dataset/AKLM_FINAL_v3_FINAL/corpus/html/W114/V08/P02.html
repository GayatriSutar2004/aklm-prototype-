Pricing And Commercial Terms

Reference UTI-06-193-08-02-11151 governs the pricing and commercial terms for Harbor Utilities West. The connection workflow supports reconnection and records service request information under the West operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 56 units and a listed service value of 144 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 198 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-06-193-08-02-11151.
