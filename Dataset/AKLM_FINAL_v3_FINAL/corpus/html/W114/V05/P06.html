Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-06-193-05-06-09376 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Utilities West. The account workflow supports connection and records bill information under the West operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 19 units and a listed service value of 53 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 52 minutes. For billing, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-06-193-05-06-09376.
