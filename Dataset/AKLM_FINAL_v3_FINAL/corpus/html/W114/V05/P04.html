Support Procedure

Reference UTI-06-193-05-04-08750 governs the support procedure for Harbor Utilities West. The bill workflow supports inspection and records inspection information under the West operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 25 units and a listed service value of 269 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 150 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-06-193-05-04-08750.
