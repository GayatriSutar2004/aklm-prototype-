Timing And Scheduling

Reference UTI-06-193-02-07-06662 governs the timing and scheduling for Harbor Utilities West. The bill workflow supports disconnection and records service request information under the West operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 70 units and a listed service value of 286 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 53 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-06-193-02-07-06662.
