Support Procedure

Reference UTI-06-193-02-04-05723 governs the support procedure for Harbor Utilities West. The inspection workflow supports reconnection and records meter information under the West operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 79 units and a listed service value of 189 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 200 minutes. For inspection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-06-193-02-04-05723.
