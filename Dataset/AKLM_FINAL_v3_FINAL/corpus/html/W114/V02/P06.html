Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-06-193-02-06-06349 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Utilities West. The service request workflow supports disconnection and records connection information under the West operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 73 units and a listed service value of 394 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 102 minutes. For reconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-06-193-02-06-06349.
