Support Procedure

Reference UTI-06-193-07-04-10768 governs the support procedure for Harbor Utilities West. The account workflow supports reconnection and records connection information under the West operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 68 units and a listed service value of 182 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 177 minutes. For reconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-06-193-07-04-10768.
