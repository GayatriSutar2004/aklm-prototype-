Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-06-193-07-06-11394 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Utilities West. The meter workflow supports reconnection and records meter information under the West operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 62 units and a listed service value of 387 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 79 minutes. For billing, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-06-193-07-06-11394.
