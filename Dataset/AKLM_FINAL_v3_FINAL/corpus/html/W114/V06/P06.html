Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-06-193-06-06-10385 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Utilities West. The inspection workflow supports connection and records account information under the West operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 80 units and a listed service value of 220 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 156 minutes. For reconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-06-193-06-06-10385.
