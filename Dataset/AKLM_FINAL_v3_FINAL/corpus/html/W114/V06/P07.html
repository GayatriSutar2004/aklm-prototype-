Timing And Scheduling

Reference UTI-06-193-06-07-10698 governs the timing and scheduling for Harbor Utilities West. The meter workflow supports reconnection and records bill information under the West operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 77 units and a listed service value of 112 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 107 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-06-193-06-07-10698.
