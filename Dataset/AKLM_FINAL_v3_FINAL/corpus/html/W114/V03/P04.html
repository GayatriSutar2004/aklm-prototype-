Support Procedure

Reference UTI-06-193-03-04-06732 governs the support procedure for Harbor Utilities West. The meter workflow supports disconnection and records bill information under the West operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 61 units and a listed service value of 356 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 123 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-06-193-03-04-06732.
