Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-06-193-03-06-07358 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Utilities West. The bill workflow supports billing and records inspection information under the West operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 55 units and a listed service value of 140 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 25 minutes. For inspection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-06-193-03-06-07358.
