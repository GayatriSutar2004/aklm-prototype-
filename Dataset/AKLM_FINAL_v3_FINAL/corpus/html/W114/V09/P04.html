Support Procedure

Reference UTI-06-193-09-04-12786 governs the support procedure for Harbor Utilities West. The meter workflow supports disconnection and records meter information under the West operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 32 units and a listed service value of 95 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 23 minutes. For reconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-06-193-09-04-12786.
