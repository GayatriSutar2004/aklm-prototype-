Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-06-193-09-06-13412 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Utilities West. The bill workflow supports billing and records connection information under the West operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 26 units and a listed service value of 300 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 106 minutes. For billing, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-06-193-09-06-13412.
