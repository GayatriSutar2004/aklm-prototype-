Pricing And Commercial Terms

Reference UTI-06-193-04-02-07115 governs the pricing and commercial terms for Harbor Utilities West. The inspection workflow supports billing and records service request information under the West operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 49 units and a listed service value of 318 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 144 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-06-193-04-02-07115.
