Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-06-193-04-06-08367 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Utilities West. The connection workflow supports inspection and records meter information under the West operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 37 units and a listed service value of 307 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 129 minutes. For reconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-06-193-04-06-08367.
