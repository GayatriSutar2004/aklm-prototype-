Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-06-783-08-06-25294 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Travel East. The reservation workflow supports transfer and records luggage information under the East operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 72 units and a listed service value of 171 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 135 minutes. For transfer, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-06-783-08-06-25294.
