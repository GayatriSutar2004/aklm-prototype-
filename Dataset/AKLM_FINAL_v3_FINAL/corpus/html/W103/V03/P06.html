Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-06-783-03-06-20249 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Travel East. The itinerary workflow supports modification and records reservation information under the East operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 83 units and a listed service value of 178 units. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-06-783-03-06-20249.
