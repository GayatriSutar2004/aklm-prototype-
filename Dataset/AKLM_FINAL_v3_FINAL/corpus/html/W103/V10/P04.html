Support Procedure

Reference TRA-06-783-10-04-26686 governs the support procedure for Harbor Travel East. The reservation workflow supports modification and records transfer information under the East operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 42 units and a listed service value of 300 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 79 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-06-783-10-04-26686.
