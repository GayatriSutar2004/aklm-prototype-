Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-06-783-10-06-27312 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Travel East. The luggage workflow supports check-in and records reservation information under the East operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 36 units and a listed service value of 84 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 162 minutes. For transfer, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-06-783-10-06-27312.
