Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-06-783-05-06-22267 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Travel East. The transfer workflow supports cancellation and records transfer information under the East operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 47 units and a listed service value of 91 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 185 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-06-783-05-06-22267.
