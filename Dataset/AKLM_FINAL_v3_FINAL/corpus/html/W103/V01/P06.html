Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-06-783-01-06-18231 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Travel East. The route workflow supports booking and records luggage information under the East operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 40 units and a listed service value of 265 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 131 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-06-783-01-06-18231.
