Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-06-783-07-06-24285 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Travel East. The route workflow supports booking and records itinerary information under the East operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 90 units and a listed service value of 425 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 31 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-06-783-07-06-24285.
