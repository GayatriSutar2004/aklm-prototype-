Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-06-783-04-06-21258 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Travel East. The luggage workflow supports check-in and records itinerary information under the East operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 65 units and a listed service value of 345 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 81 minutes. For modification, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-06-783-04-06-21258.
