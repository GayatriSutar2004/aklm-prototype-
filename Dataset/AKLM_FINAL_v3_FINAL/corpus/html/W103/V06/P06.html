Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-06-783-06-06-23276 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Travel East. The boarding pass workflow supports booking and records route information under the East operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 29 units and a listed service value of 258 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 108 minutes. For modification, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-06-783-06-06-23276.
