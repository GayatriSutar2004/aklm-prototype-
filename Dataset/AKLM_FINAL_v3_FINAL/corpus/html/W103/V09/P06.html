Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-06-783-09-06-26303 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Travel East. The itinerary workflow supports modification and records boarding pass information under the East operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 54 units and a listed service value of 338 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 58 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-06-783-09-06-26303.
