Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-06-783-02-06-19240 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Travel East. The reservation workflow supports transfer and records boarding pass information under the East operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 22 units and a listed service value of 432 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 54 minutes. For modification, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-06-783-02-06-19240.
