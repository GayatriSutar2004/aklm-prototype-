Eligibility And Requirements

Reference FIN-04-411-01-06-25228 governs the eligibility and requirements for Cedar Finance Coastal. The account workflow supports dispute and records account information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 48 units and a listed service value of 278 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 167 minutes. For dispute, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-04-411-01-06-25228.
