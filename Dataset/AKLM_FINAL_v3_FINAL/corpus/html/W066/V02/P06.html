Eligibility And Requirements

Reference FIN-04-411-02-06-26237 governs the eligibility and requirements for Cedar Finance Coastal. The transfer workflow supports transfer and records transfer information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 30 units and a listed service value of 445 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 90 minutes. For reconciliation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-04-411-02-06-26237.
