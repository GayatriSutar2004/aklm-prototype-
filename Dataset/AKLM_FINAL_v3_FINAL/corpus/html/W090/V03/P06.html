Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-05-302-03-06-17302 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Public Service Lakeside. The appointment workflow supports verification and records certificate information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 73 units and a listed service value of 376 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 200 minutes. For appeal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-05-302-03-06-17302.
