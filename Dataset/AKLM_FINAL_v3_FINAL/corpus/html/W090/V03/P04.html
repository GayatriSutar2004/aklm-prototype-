Support Procedure

Reference PUB-05-302-03-04-16676 governs the support procedure for Solstice Public Service Lakeside. The application workflow supports appeal and records permit information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 79 units and a listed service value of 171 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 117 minutes. For renewal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-05-302-03-04-16676.
