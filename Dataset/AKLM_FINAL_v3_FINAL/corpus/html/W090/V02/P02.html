Pricing And Commercial Terms

Reference PUB-05-302-02-02-15041 governs the pricing and commercial terms for Solstice Public Service Lakeside. The request workflow supports renewal and records case information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 24 units and a listed service value of 220 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 111 minutes. For renewal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-05-302-02-02-15041.
