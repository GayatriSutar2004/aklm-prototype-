Support Procedure

Reference PUB-05-302-06-04-19703 governs the support procedure for Solstice Public Service Lakeside. The request workflow supports review and records request information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 25 units and a listed service value of 251 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 67 minutes. For verification, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-05-302-06-04-19703.
