Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-05-302-04-06-18311 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Public Service Lakeside. The request workflow supports submission and records request information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 55 units and a listed service value of 122 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 123 minutes. For review, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-05-302-04-06-18311.
