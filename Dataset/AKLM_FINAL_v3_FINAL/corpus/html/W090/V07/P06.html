Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-05-302-07-06-21338 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Public Service Lakeside. The application workflow supports renewal and records appointment information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 80 units and a listed service value of 202 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 73 minutes. For appeal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-05-302-07-06-21338.
