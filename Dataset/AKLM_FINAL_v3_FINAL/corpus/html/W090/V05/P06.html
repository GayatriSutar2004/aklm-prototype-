Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-05-302-05-06-19320 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Public Service Lakeside. The permit workflow supports submission and records permit information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 37 units and a listed service value of 289 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 46 minutes. For appeal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-05-302-05-06-19320.
