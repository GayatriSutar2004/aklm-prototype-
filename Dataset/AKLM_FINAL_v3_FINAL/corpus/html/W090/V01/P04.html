Support Procedure

Reference PUB-05-302-01-04-14658 governs the support procedure for Solstice Public Service Lakeside. The permit workflow supports review and records application information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 36 units and a listed service value of 258 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 90 minutes. For renewal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-05-302-01-04-14658.
