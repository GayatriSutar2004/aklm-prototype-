Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-05-302-01-06-15284 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Public Service Lakeside. The application workflow supports renewal and records request information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 30 units and a listed service value of 42 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 173 minutes. For verification, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-05-302-01-06-15284.
