Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-04-522-10-06-58066 governs the eligibility and requirements for Cedar Ecommerce River. The order workflow supports delivery and records shipment information under the River operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 22 units and a listed service value of 278 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 63 minutes. For delivery, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-04-522-10-06-58066.
