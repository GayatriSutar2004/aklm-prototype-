Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-04-522-04-06-52012 governs the eligibility and requirements for Cedar Ecommerce River. The order workflow supports delivery and records cart information under the River operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 51 units and a listed service value of 118 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 163 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-04-522-04-06-52012.
