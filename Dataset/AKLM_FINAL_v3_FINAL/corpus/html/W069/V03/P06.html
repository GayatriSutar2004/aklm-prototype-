Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-04-522-03-06-51003 governs the eligibility and requirements for Cedar Ecommerce River. The invoice workflow supports cancellation and records shipment information under the River operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 69 units and a listed service value of 372 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 59 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-04-522-03-06-51003.
