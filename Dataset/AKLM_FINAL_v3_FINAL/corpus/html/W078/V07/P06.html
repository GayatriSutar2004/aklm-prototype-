Eligibility And Requirements

Reference LOG-04-855-07-06-26310 governs the eligibility and requirements for Cedar Logistics Highland. The shipment workflow supports delivery and records delivery information under the Highland operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 89 units and a listed service value of 320 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 70 minutes. For delivery, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference LOG-04-855-07-06-26310.
