Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-04-559-01-06-56904 governs the eligibility and requirements for Cedar Public Service Lakeside. The appointment workflow supports appeal and records case information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 45 units and a listed service value of 379 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 168 minutes. For renewal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-04-559-01-06-56904.
