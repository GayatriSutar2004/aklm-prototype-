Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-04-559-02-06-57913 governs the eligibility and requirements for Cedar Public Service Lakeside. The request workflow supports verification and records certificate information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 27 units and a listed service value of 125 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 91 minutes. For verification, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-04-559-02-06-57913.
