Eligibility And Requirements

Reference INS-01-222-01-06-58007 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Insurance Metro. The renewal workflow supports verification and records renewal information under the Metro operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 33 units and a listed service value of 367 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 107 minutes. For renewal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference INS-01-222-01-06-58007.
