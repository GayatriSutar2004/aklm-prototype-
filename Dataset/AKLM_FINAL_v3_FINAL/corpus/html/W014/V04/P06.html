Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-01-481-04-06-16467 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Utilities West. The service request workflow supports disconnection and records connection information under the West operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 33 units and a listed service value of 308 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 104 minutes. For reconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-01-481-04-06-16467.
