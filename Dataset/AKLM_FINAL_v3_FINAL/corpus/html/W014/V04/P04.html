Support Procedure

Reference UTI-01-481-04-04-15841 governs the support procedure for Atlas Utilities West. The inspection workflow supports disconnection and records service request information under the West operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 39 units and a listed service value of 103 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 21 minutes. For connection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-01-481-04-04-15841.
