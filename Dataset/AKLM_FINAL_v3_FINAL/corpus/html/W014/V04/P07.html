Timing And Scheduling

Reference UTI-01-481-04-07-16780 governs the timing and scheduling for Atlas Utilities West. The bill workflow supports billing and records bill information under the West operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 30 units and a listed service value of 200 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 55 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-01-481-04-07-16780.
