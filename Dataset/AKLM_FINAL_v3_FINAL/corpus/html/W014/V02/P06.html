Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-01-481-02-06-14449 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Utilities West. The inspection workflow supports reconnection and records meter information under the West operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 69 units and a listed service value of 395 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 77 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-01-481-02-06-14449.
