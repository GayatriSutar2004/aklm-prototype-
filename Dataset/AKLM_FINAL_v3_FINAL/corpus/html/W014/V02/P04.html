Support Procedure

Reference UTI-01-481-02-04-13823 governs the support procedure for Atlas Utilities West. The connection workflow supports connection and records connection information under the West operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 75 units and a listed service value of 190 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 175 minutes. For connection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-01-481-02-04-13823.
