Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-01-481-10-06-22521 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Utilities West. The service request workflow supports disconnection and records bill information under the West operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 83 units and a listed service value of 47 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 185 minutes. For reconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-01-481-10-06-22521.
