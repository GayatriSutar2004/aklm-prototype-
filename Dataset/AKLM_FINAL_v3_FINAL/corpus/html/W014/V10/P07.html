Timing And Scheduling

Reference UTI-01-481-10-07-22834 governs the timing and scheduling for Atlas Utilities West. The bill workflow supports billing and records meter information under the West operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 80 units and a listed service value of 360 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 136 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-01-481-10-07-22834.
