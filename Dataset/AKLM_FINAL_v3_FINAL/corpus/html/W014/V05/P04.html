Support Procedure

Reference UTI-01-481-05-04-16850 governs the support procedure for Atlas Utilities West. The meter workflow supports billing and records bill information under the West operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 21 units and a listed service value of 270 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 125 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-01-481-05-04-16850.
