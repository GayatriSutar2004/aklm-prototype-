Operating Policy

Reference UTI-01-481-01-03-12501 governs the operating policy for Atlas Utilities West. The service request workflow supports inspection and records connection information under the West operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 17 units and a listed service value of 131 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 120 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-01-481-01-03-12501.
