Pricing And Commercial Terms

Reference UTI-01-481-01-02-12188 governs the pricing and commercial terms for Atlas Utilities West. The meter workflow supports inspection and records account information under the West operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 20 units and a listed service value of 239 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 169 minutes. For reconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-01-481-01-02-12188.
