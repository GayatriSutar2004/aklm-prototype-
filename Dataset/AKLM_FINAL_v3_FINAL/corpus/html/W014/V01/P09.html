Available Services

Reference UTI-01-481-01-09-14379 governs the available services for Atlas Utilities West. The service request workflow supports reconnection and records meter information under the West operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 78 units and a listed service value of 325 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 188 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-01-481-01-09-14379.
