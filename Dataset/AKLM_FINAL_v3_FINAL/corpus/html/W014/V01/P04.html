Support Procedure

Reference UTI-01-481-01-04-12814 governs the support procedure for Atlas Utilities West. The bill workflow supports inspection and records service request information under the West operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 14 units and a listed service value of 444 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 71 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-01-481-01-04-12814.
