Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-01-481-09-06-21512 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Utilities West. The meter workflow supports disconnection and records meter information under the West operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 22 units and a listed service value of 301 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 81 minutes. For billing, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-01-481-09-06-21512.
