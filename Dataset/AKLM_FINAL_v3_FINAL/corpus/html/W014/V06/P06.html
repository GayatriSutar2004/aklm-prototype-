Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-01-481-06-06-18485 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Utilities West. The connection workflow supports inspection and records service request information under the West operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 76 units and a listed service value of 221 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 131 minutes. For reconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-01-481-06-06-18485.
