Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-01-481-08-06-20503 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Utilities West. The inspection workflow supports reconnection and records account information under the West operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 40 units and a listed service value of 134 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 158 minutes. For reconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-01-481-08-06-20503.
