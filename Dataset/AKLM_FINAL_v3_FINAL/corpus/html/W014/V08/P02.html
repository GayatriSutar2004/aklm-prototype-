Pricing And Commercial Terms

Reference UTI-01-481-08-02-19251 governs the pricing and commercial terms for Atlas Utilities West. The service request workflow supports connection and records inspection information under the West operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 52 units and a listed service value of 145 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 173 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-01-481-08-02-19251.
