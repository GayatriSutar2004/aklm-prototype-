Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-08-491-05-06-86541 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Ecommerce River. The invoice workflow supports purchase and records invoice information under the River operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 52 units and a listed service value of 200 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 106 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-08-491-05-06-86541.
