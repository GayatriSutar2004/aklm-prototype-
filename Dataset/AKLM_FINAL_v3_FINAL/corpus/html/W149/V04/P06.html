Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-08-491-04-06-85532 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Ecommerce River. The cart workflow supports return and records shipment information under the River operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 70 units and a listed service value of 454 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 183 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-08-491-04-06-85532.
