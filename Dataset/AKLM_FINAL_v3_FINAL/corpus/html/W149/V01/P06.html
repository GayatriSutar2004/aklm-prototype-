Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-08-491-01-06-82505 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Ecommerce River. The product workflow supports cancellation and records cart information under the River operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 45 units and a listed service value of 374 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 52 minutes. For return, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-08-491-01-06-82505.
