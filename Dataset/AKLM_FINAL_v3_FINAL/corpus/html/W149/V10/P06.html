Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-08-491-10-06-91586 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Ecommerce River. The cart workflow supports return and records return information under the River operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 41 units and a listed service value of 193 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 83 minutes. For purchase, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-08-491-10-06-91586.
