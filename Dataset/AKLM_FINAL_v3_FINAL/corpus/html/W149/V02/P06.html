Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-08-491-02-06-83514 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Ecommerce River. The return workflow supports delivery and records order information under the River operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 27 units and a listed service value of 120 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 156 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-08-491-02-06-83514.
