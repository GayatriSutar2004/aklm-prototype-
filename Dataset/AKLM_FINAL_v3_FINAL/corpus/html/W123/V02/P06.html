Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-07-526-02-06-77620 governs the eligibility and requirements for Pioneer Travel East. The boarding pass workflow supports booking and records luggage information under the East operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 86 units and a listed service value of 95 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 59 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-07-526-02-06-77620.
