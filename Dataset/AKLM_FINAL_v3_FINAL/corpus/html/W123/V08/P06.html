Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-07-526-08-06-83674 governs the eligibility and requirements for Pioneer Travel East. The boarding pass workflow supports transfer and records reservation information under the East operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 57 units and a listed service value of 255 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 140 minutes. For check-in, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-07-526-08-06-83674.
