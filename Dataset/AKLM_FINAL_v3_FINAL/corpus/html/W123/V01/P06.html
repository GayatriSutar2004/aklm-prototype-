Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-07-526-01-06-76611 governs the eligibility and requirements for Pioneer Travel East. The transfer workflow supports booking and records reservation information under the East operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 25 units and a listed service value of 349 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 136 minutes. For modification, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-07-526-01-06-76611.
