Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-07-526-07-06-82665 governs the eligibility and requirements for Pioneer Travel East. The transfer workflow supports booking and records route information under the East operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 75 units and a listed service value of 88 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 36 minutes. For transfer, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-07-526-07-06-82665.
