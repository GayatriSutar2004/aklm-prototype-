Eligibility And Requirements

Reference MED-03-189-01-06-77714 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Media Lakeside. The subscription workflow supports sharing and records subscription information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 13 units and a listed service value of 337 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 75 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference MED-03-189-01-06-77714.
