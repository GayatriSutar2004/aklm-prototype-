Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-01-333-10-06-90845 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Public Service Lakeside. The case workflow supports appeal and records appointment information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 86 units and a listed service value of 367 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 184 minutes. For renewal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-01-333-10-06-90845.
