Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-01-333-03-06-83782 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Public Service Lakeside. The permit workflow supports renewal and records appointment information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 54 units and a listed service value of 40 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 180 minutes. For review, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-01-333-03-06-83782.
