Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-01-333-04-06-84791 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Public Service Lakeside. The case workflow supports renewal and records permit information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 36 units and a listed service value of 207 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 103 minutes. For appeal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-01-333-04-06-84791.
