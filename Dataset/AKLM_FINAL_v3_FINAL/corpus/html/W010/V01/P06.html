Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-01-333-01-06-81764 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Public Service Lakeside. The appointment workflow supports submission and records case information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 90 units and a listed service value of 127 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 153 minutes. For review, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-01-333-01-06-81764.
