Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-01-333-02-06-82773 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Public Service Lakeside. The request workflow supports review and records application information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 72 units and a listed service value of 294 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 76 minutes. For appeal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-01-333-02-06-82773.
