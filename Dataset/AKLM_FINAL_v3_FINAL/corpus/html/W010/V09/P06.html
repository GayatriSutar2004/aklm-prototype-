Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PUB-01-333-09-06-89836 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Public Service Lakeside. The permit workflow supports renewal and records application information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 25 units and a listed service value of 200 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 80 minutes. For submission, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-01-333-09-06-89836.
