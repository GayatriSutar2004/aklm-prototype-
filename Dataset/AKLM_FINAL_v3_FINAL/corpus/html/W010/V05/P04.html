Support Procedure

Reference PUB-01-333-05-04-85174 governs the support procedure for Atlas Public Service Lakeside. The permit workflow supports appeal and records request information under the Lakeside operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 24 units and a listed service value of 169 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 124 minutes. For verification, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PUB-01-333-05-04-85174.
