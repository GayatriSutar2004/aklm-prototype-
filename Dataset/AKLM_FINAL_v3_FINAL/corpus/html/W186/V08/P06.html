Eligibility And Requirements

Reference FIN-10-863-08-06-82571 governs the eligibility and requirements for Aurora Finance Coastal. The transfer workflow supports verification and records transfer information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 69 units and a listed service value of 267 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 20 minutes. For closure, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-10-863-08-06-82571.
