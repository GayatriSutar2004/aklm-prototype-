Eligibility And Requirements

Reference NON-06-378-01-06-44935 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Nonprofit River. The application workflow supports registration and records application information under the River operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 28 units and a listed service value of 248 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 135 minutes. For allocation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference NON-06-378-01-06-44935.
