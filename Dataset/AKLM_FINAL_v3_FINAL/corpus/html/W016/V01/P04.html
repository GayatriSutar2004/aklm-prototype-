Support Procedure

Reference HR-01-555-01-04-28652 governs the support procedure for Atlas Hr Coastal. The benefit workflow supports appeal and records leave request information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 52 units and a listed service value of 284 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 162 minutes. For enrolment, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HR-01-555-01-04-28652.
