Support Procedure

Reference HEA-05-117-08-04-82126 governs the support procedure for Solstice Healthcare Central. The care plan workflow supports rescheduling and records membership information under the Central operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 52 units and a listed service value of 143 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 138 minutes. For rescheduling, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-05-117-08-04-82126.
