Support Procedure

Reference HEA-05-117-06-04-80108 governs the support procedure for Solstice Healthcare Central. The session workflow supports cancellation and records care plan information under the Central operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 88 units and a listed service value of 230 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 111 minutes. For rescheduling, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-05-117-06-04-80108.
