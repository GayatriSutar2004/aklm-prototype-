Support Procedure

Reference HEA-05-117-10-04-84144 governs the support procedure for Solstice Healthcare Central. The appointment workflow supports review and records care plan information under the Central operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 16 units and a listed service value of 56 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 165 minutes. For rescheduling, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-05-117-10-04-84144.
