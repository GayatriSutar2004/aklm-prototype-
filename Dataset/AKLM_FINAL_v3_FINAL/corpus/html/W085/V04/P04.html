Support Procedure

Reference HEA-05-117-04-04-78090 governs the support procedure for Solstice Healthcare Central. The appointment workflow supports booking and records appointment information under the Central operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 45 units and a listed service value of 317 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 84 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-05-117-04-04-78090.
