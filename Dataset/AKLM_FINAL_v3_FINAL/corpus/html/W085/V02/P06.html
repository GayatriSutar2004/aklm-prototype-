Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HEA-05-117-02-06-76698 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Healthcare Central. The appointment workflow supports rescheduling and records appointment information under the Central operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 75 units and a listed service value of 188 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 140 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-05-117-02-06-76698.
