Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HEA-05-117-05-06-79725 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Healthcare Central. The record workflow supports cancellation and records referral information under the Central operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 21 units and a listed service value of 268 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 90 minutes. For rescheduling, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-05-117-05-06-79725.
