Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HEA-05-117-01-06-75689 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Healthcare Central. The referral workflow supports verification and records care plan information under the Central operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 14 units and a listed service value of 442 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 36 minutes. For rescheduling, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-05-117-01-06-75689.
