Timing And Scheduling

Reference HEA-05-117-01-07-76002 governs the timing and scheduling for Solstice Healthcare Central. The appointment workflow supports rescheduling and records session information under the Central operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 90 units and a listed service value of 334 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 168 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-05-117-01-07-76002.
