Support Procedure

Reference HEA-05-117-03-04-77081 governs the support procedure for Solstice Healthcare Central. The referral workflow supports booking and records care plan information under the Central operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 63 units and a listed service value of 150 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 161 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-05-117-03-04-77081.
