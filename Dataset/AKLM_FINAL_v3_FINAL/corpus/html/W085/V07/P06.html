Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HEA-05-117-07-06-81743 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Healthcare Central. The referral workflow supports rescheduling and records session information under the Central operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 64 units and a listed service value of 181 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 117 minutes. For rescheduling, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-05-117-07-06-81743.
