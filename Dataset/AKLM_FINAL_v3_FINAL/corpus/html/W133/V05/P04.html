Support Procedure

Reference TEL-07-896-05-04-59211 governs the support procedure for Pioneer Telecom East. The SIM workflow supports activation and records support request information under the East operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 70 units and a listed service value of 433 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 200 minutes. For suspension, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TEL-07-896-05-04-59211.
