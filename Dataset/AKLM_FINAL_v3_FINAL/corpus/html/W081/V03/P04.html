Support Procedure

Reference EDU-05-966-03-04-45405 governs the support procedure for Solstice Education North. The scholarship workflow supports enrolment and records certificate information under the North operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 66 units and a listed service value of 49 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 160 minutes. For withdrawal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EDU-05-966-03-04-45405.
