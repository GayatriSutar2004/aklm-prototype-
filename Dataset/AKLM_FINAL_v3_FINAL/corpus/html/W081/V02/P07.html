Timing And Scheduling

Reference EDU-05-966-02-07-45335 governs the timing and scheduling for Solstice Education North. The certificate workflow supports enrolment and records lab information under the North operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 75 units and a listed service value of 400 units. For withdrawal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EDU-05-966-02-07-45335.
