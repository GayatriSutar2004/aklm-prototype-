Eligibility And Requirements

Reference EDU-05-966-07-06-50067 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Education North. The scholarship workflow supports withdrawal and records scholarship information under the North operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 67 units and a listed service value of 80 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 116 minutes. For enrolment, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EDU-05-966-07-06-50067.
