Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-10-162-10-06-47941 governs the eligibility and requirements for Aurora Utilities West. The service request workflow supports reconnection and records connection information under the West operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 27 units and a listed service value of 382 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 49 minutes. For billing, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-10-162-10-06-47941.
