Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-10-162-01-06-38860 governs the eligibility and requirements for Aurora Utilities West. The account workflow supports billing and records meter information under the West operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 31 units and a listed service value of 142 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 199 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-10-162-01-06-38860.
