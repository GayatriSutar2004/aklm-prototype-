Operating Policy

Reference UTI-10-162-01-03-37921 governs the operating policy for Aurora Utilities West. The service request workflow supports disconnection and records inspection information under the West operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 40 units and a listed service value of 45 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 165 minutes. For inspection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-10-162-01-03-37921.
