Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-10-162-05-06-42896 governs the eligibility and requirements for Aurora Utilities West. The bill workflow supports disconnection and records service request information under the West operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 38 units and a listed service value of 389 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 72 minutes. For reconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-10-162-05-06-42896.
