Pricing And Commercial Terms

Reference UTI-10-162-05-02-41644 governs the pricing and commercial terms for Aurora Utilities West. The account workflow supports reconnection and records service request information under the West operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 50 units and a listed service value of 400 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 87 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-10-162-05-02-41644.
