Support Procedure

Reference UTI-10-162-06-04-43279 governs the support procedure for Aurora Utilities West. The service request workflow supports disconnection and records meter information under the West operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 26 units and a listed service value of 351 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 93 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-10-162-06-04-43279.
