Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-10-162-03-06-40878 governs the eligibility and requirements for Aurora Utilities West. The meter workflow supports connection and records connection information under the West operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 74 units and a listed service value of 55 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 45 minutes. For reconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-10-162-03-06-40878.
