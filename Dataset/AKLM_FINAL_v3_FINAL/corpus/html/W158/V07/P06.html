Eligibility And Requirements

Reference LOG-08-824-07-06-59830 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Logistics Highland. The parcel workflow supports pickup and records pickup information under the Highland operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 29 units and a listed service value of 235 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 90 minutes. For pickup, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference LOG-08-824-07-06-59830.
