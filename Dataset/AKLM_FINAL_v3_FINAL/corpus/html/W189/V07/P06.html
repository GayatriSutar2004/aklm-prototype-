Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-10-974-07-06-05319 governs the eligibility and requirements for Aurora Ecommerce River. The shipment workflow supports purchase and records invoice information under the River operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 65 units and a listed service value of 281 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 143 minutes. For delivery, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-10-974-07-06-05319.
