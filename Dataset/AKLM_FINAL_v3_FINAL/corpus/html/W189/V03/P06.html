Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-10-974-03-06-01283 governs the eligibility and requirements for Aurora Ecommerce River. The invoice workflow supports cancellation and records cart information under the River operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 58 units and a listed service value of 455 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 89 minutes. For delivery, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-10-974-03-06-01283.
