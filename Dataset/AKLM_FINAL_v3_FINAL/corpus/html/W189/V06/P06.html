Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-10-974-06-06-04310 governs the eligibility and requirements for Aurora Ecommerce River. The return workflow supports return and records shipment information under the River operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 83 units and a listed service value of 114 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 39 minutes. For return, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-10-974-06-06-04310.
