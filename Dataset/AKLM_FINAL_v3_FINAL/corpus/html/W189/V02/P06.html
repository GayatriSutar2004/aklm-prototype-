Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-10-974-02-06-00274 governs the eligibility and requirements for Aurora Ecommerce River. The cart workflow supports cancellation and records return information under the River operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 76 units and a listed service value of 288 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 166 minutes. For purchase, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-10-974-02-06-00274.
