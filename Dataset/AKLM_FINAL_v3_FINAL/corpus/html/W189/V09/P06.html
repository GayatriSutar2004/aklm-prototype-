Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-10-974-09-06-07337 governs the eligibility and requirements for Aurora Ecommerce River. The invoice workflow supports delivery and records return information under the River operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 29 units and a listed service value of 194 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 170 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-10-974-09-06-07337.
