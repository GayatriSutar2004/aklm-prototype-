Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-10-974-08-06-06328 governs the eligibility and requirements for Aurora Ecommerce River. The cart workflow supports cancellation and records order information under the River operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 47 units and a listed service value of 448 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 66 minutes. For return, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-10-974-08-06-06328.
