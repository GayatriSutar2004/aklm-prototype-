Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-10-974-01-06-99265 governs the eligibility and requirements for Aurora Ecommerce River. The shipment workflow supports purchase and records order information under the River operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 15 units and a listed service value of 121 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 62 minutes. For delivery, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-10-974-01-06-99265.
