Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TEL-02-187-01-06-63901 governs the eligibility and requirements for Nimbus Telecom East. The support request workflow supports activation and records support request information under the East operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 53 units and a listed service value of 392 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 23 minutes. For activation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TEL-02-187-01-06-63901.
