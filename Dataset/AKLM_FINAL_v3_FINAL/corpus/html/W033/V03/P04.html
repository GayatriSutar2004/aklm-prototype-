Support Procedure

Reference TEL-02-187-03-04-65293 governs the support procedure for Nimbus Telecom East. The support request workflow supports transfer and records support request information under the East operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 23 units and a listed service value of 100 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 148 minutes. For suspension, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TEL-02-187-03-04-65293.
