Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-09-012-04-06-96398 governs the eligibility and requirements for Summit Travel East. The luggage workflow supports transfer and records luggage information under the East operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 20 units and a listed service value of 176 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 96 minutes. For check-in, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-09-012-04-06-96398.
