Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-09-012-10-06-02452 governs the eligibility and requirements for Summit Travel East. The luggage workflow supports transfer and records reservation information under the East operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 70 units and a listed service value of 336 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 177 minutes. For modification, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-09-012-10-06-02452.
