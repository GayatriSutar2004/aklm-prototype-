Security And Access

Reference TRA-09-012-10-08-03078 governs the security and access for Summit Travel East. The boarding pass workflow supports transfer and records transfer information under the East operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 64 units and a listed service value of 120 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 79 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-09-012-10-08-03078.
