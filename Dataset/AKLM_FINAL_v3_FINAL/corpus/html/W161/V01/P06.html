Eligibility And Requirements

Reference EDU-09-935-01-06-77533 governs the eligibility and requirements for Summit Education North. The module workflow supports verification and records scholarship information under the North operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 36 units and a listed service value of 256 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 55 minutes. For withdrawal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EDU-09-935-01-06-77533.
