Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-04-707-06-06-93625 governs the eligibility and requirements for Cedar Utilities West. The connection workflow supports disconnection and records service request information under the West operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 31 units and a listed service value of 52 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 146 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-04-707-06-06-93625.
