Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-04-707-02-06-89589 governs the eligibility and requirements for Cedar Utilities West. The inspection workflow supports billing and records service request information under the West operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 24 units and a listed service value of 226 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 92 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-04-707-02-06-89589.
