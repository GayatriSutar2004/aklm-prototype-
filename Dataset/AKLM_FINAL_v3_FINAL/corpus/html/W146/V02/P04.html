Support Procedure

Reference FIN-08-380-02-04-59131 governs the support procedure for Lumen Finance Coastal. The beneficiary workflow supports verification and records beneficiary information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 55 units and a listed service value of 155 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 27 minutes. For closure, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-08-380-02-04-59131.
