Support Procedure

Reference FIN-08-380-06-04-63167 governs the support procedure for Lumen Finance Coastal. The transfer workflow supports transfer and records payment information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 62 units and a listed service value of 402 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 81 minutes. For reconciliation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-08-380-06-04-63167.
