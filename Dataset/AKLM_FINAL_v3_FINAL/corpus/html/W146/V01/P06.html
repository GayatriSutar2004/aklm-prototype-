Eligibility And Requirements

Reference FIN-08-380-01-06-58748 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Finance Coastal. The payment workflow supports verification and records payment information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 67 units and a listed service value of 193 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 187 minutes. For reconciliation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-08-380-01-06-58748.
