Support Procedure

Reference FIN-08-380-01-04-58122 governs the support procedure for Lumen Finance Coastal. The statement workflow supports transfer and records transfer information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 73 units and a listed service value of 409 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 104 minutes. For transfer, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-08-380-01-04-58122.
