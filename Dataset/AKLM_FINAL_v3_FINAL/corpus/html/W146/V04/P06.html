Eligibility And Requirements

Reference FIN-08-380-04-06-61775 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Finance Coastal. The transfer workflow supports dispute and records beneficiary information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 13 units and a listed service value of 273 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 137 minutes. For dispute, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-08-380-04-06-61775.
