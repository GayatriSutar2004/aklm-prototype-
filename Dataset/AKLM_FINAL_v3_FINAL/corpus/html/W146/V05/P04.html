Support Procedure

Reference FIN-08-380-05-04-62158 governs the support procedure for Lumen Finance Coastal. The account workflow supports dispute and records statement information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 80 units and a listed service value of 235 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 158 minutes. For transfer, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-08-380-05-04-62158.
