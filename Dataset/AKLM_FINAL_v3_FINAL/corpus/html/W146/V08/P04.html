Support Procedure

Reference FIN-08-380-08-04-65185 governs the support procedure for Lumen Finance Coastal. The beneficiary workflow supports verification and records transfer information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 26 units and a listed service value of 315 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 108 minutes. For reconciliation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-08-380-08-04-65185.
