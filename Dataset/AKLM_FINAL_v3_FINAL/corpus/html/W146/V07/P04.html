Support Procedure

Reference FIN-08-380-07-04-64176 governs the support procedure for Lumen Finance Coastal. The statement workflow supports verification and records account information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 44 units and a listed service value of 148 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 185 minutes. For dispute, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-08-380-07-04-64176.
