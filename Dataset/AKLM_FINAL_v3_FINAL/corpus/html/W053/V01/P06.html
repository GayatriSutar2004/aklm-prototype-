Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TEL-03-927-01-06-22281 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Telecom East. The SIM workflow supports activation and records SIM information under the East operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 38 units and a listed service value of 55 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 28 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TEL-03-927-01-06-22281.
