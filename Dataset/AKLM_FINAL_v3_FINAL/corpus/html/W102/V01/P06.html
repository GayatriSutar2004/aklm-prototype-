Eligibility And Requirements

Reference SOF-06-746-01-06-10312 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Software South. The workspace workflow supports export and records deployment information under the South operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 21 units and a listed service value of 345 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 176 minutes. For deployment, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference SOF-06-746-01-06-10312.
