Eligibility And Requirements

Reference FIN-02-925-05-06-12504 governs the eligibility and requirements for Nimbus Finance Coastal. The statement workflow supports dispute and records payment information under the Coastal operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 85 units and a listed service value of 357 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 30 minutes. For transfer, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FIN-02-925-05-06-12504.
