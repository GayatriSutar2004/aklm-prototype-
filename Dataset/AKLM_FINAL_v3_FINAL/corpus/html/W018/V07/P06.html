Eligibility And Requirements

Reference LOG-01-629-07-06-51170 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Logistics Highland. The shipment workflow supports pickup and records pickup information under the Highland operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 55 units and a listed service value of 68 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 55 minutes. For claim, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference LOG-01-629-07-06-51170.
