Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-06-008-01-06-65745 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Ecommerce River. The invoice workflow supports replacement and records return information under the River operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 75 units and a listed service value of 206 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 42 minutes. For purchase, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-06-008-01-06-65745.
