Timing And Scheduling

Reference ECO-06-008-09-07-74130 governs the timing and scheduling for Harbor Ecommerce River. The return workflow supports purchase and records return information under the River operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 86 units and a listed service value of 171 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 101 minutes. For purchase, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-06-008-09-07-74130.
