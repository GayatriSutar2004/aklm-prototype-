Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-06-008-09-06-73817 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Ecommerce River. The product workflow supports return and records shipment information under the River operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 89 units and a listed service value of 279 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 150 minutes. For return, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-06-008-09-06-73817.
