Support Procedure

Reference ECO-06-008-10-04-74200 governs the support procedure for Harbor Ecommerce River. The order workflow supports purchase and records return information under the River operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 77 units and a listed service value of 241 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 171 minutes. For return, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-06-008-10-04-74200.
