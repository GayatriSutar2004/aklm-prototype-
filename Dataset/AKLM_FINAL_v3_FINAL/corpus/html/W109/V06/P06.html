Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-06-008-06-06-70790 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Ecommerce River. The cart workflow supports delivery and records cart information under the River operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 64 units and a listed service value of 199 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 200 minutes. For delivery, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-06-008-06-06-70790.
