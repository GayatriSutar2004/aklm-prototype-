Eligibility And Requirements

Reference FOO-03-890-01-06-14362 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Food South. The delivery workflow supports delivery and records order information under the South operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 19 units and a listed service value of 135 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 73 minutes. For delivery, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference FOO-03-890-01-06-14362.
