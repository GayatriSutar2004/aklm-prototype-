Timing And Scheduling

Reference EDU-08-195-02-07-20475 governs the timing and scheduling for Lumen Education North. The certificate workflow supports attendance and records certificate information under the North operating profile. For enrolment, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EDU-08-195-02-07-20475.
