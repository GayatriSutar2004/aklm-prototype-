Support Procedure

Reference EDU-08-195-03-04-20545 governs the support procedure for Lumen Education North. The scholarship workflow supports attendance and records certificate information under the North operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 21 units and a listed service value of 301 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 175 minutes. For enrolment, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EDU-08-195-03-04-20545.
