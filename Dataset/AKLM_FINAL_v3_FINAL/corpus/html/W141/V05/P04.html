Support Procedure

Reference EDU-08-195-05-04-22563 governs the support procedure for Lumen Education North. The certificate workflow supports withdrawal and records scholarship information under the North operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 64 units and a listed service value of 214 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 21 minutes. For withdrawal, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EDU-08-195-05-04-22563.
