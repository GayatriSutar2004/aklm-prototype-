Support Procedure

Reference TRA-08-269-05-04-38401 governs the support procedure for Lumen Travel East. The transfer workflow supports modification and records transfer information under the East operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 23 units and a listed service value of 54 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 112 minutes. For transfer, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-08-269-05-04-38401.
