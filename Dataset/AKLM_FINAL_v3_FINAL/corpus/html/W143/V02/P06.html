Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-08-269-02-06-36000 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Travel East. The luggage workflow supports booking and records reservation information under the East operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 71 units and a listed service value of 179 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 64 minutes. For transfer, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-08-269-02-06-36000.
