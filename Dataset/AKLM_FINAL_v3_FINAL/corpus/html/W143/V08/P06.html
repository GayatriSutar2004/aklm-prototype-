Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-08-269-08-06-42054 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Travel East. The luggage workflow supports booking and records route information under the East operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 42 units and a listed service value of 339 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 145 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-08-269-08-06-42054.
