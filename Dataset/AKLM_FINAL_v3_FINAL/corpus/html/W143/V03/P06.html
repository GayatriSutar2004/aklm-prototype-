Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-08-269-03-06-37009 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Travel East. The transfer workflow supports transfer and records luggage information under the East operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 53 units and a listed service value of 346 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 168 minutes. For check-in, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-08-269-03-06-37009.
