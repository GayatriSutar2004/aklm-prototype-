Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-08-269-09-06-43063 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Travel East. The transfer workflow supports transfer and records reservation information under the East operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 24 units and a listed service value of 85 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 68 minutes. For check-in, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-08-269-09-06-43063.
