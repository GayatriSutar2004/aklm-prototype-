Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-08-269-10-06-44072 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Travel East. The boarding pass workflow supports modification and records luggage information under the East operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 85 units and a listed service value of 252 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 172 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-08-269-10-06-44072.
