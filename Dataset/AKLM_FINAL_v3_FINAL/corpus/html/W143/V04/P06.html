Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-08-269-04-06-38018 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Travel East. The boarding pass workflow supports transfer and records boarding pass information under the East operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 35 units and a listed service value of 92 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 91 minutes. For transfer, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-08-269-04-06-38018.
