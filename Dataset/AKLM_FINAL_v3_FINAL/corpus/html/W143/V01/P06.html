Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-08-269-01-06-34991 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Travel East. The itinerary workflow supports cancellation and records route information under the East operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 89 units and a listed service value of 433 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 141 minutes. For check-in, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-08-269-01-06-34991.
