Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-05-043-03-06-61869 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Travel East. The transfer workflow supports check-in and records itinerary information under the East operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 19 units and a listed service value of 94 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 153 minutes. For modification, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-05-043-03-06-61869.
