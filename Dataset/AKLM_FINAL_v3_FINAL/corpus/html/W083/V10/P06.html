Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-05-043-10-06-68932 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Travel East. The boarding pass workflow supports cancellation and records luggage information under the East operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 51 units and a listed service value of 421 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 157 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-05-043-10-06-68932.
