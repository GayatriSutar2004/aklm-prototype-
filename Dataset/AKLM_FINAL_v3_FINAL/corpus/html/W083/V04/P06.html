Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-05-043-04-06-62878 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Travel East. The boarding pass workflow supports cancellation and records transfer information under the East operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 80 units and a listed service value of 261 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 76 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-05-043-04-06-62878.
