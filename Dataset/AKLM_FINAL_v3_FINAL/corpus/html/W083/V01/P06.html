Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-05-043-01-06-59851 governs the eligibility and requirements for Solstice Travel East. The itinerary workflow supports transfer and records boarding pass information under the East operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 55 units and a listed service value of 181 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 126 minutes. For check-in, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-05-043-01-06-59851.
