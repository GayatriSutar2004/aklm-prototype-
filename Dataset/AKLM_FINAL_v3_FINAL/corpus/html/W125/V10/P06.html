Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HEA-07-600-10-06-01530 governs the eligibility and requirements for Pioneer Healthcare Central. The care plan workflow supports booking and records care plan information under the Central operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 59 units and a listed service value of 429 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 77 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-07-600-10-06-01530.
