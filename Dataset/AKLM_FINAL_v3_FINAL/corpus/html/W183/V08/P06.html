Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-10-752-08-06-58814 governs the eligibility and requirements for Aurora Travel East. The boarding pass workflow supports check-in and records itinerary information under the East operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 12 units and a listed service value of 86 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 155 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-10-752-08-06-58814.
