Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-10-752-01-06-51751 governs the eligibility and requirements for Aurora Travel East. The transfer workflow supports modification and records itinerary information under the East operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 59 units and a listed service value of 180 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 151 minutes. For modification, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-10-752-01-06-51751.
