Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-08-713-10-06-39100 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Automotive Central. The part workflow supports replacement and records service booking information under the Central operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 76 units and a listed service value of 134 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 175 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-08-713-10-06-39100.
