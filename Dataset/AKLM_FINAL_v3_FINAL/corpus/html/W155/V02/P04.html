Support Procedure

Reference AUT-08-713-02-04-30402 governs the support procedure for Lumen Automotive Central. The vehicle workflow supports maintenance and records warranty information under the Central operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 68 units and a listed service value of 277 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 165 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-08-713-02-04-30402.
