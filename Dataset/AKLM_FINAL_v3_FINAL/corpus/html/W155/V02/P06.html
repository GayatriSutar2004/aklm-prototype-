Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-08-713-02-06-31028 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Automotive Central. The warranty workflow supports maintenance and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 62 units and a listed service value of 61 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 67 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-08-713-02-06-31028.
