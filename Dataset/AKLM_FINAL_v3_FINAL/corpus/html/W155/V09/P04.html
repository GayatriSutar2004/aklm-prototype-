Support Procedure

Reference AUT-08-713-09-04-37465 governs the support procedure for Lumen Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports booking and records warranty information under the Central operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 21 units and a listed service value of 183 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 169 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-08-713-09-04-37465.
