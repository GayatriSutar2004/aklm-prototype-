Support Procedure

Reference AUT-08-713-07-04-35447 governs the support procedure for Lumen Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports claim and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 57 units and a listed service value of 270 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 142 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-08-713-07-04-35447.
