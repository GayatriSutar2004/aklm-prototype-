Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-08-713-07-06-36073 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports maintenance and records warranty information under the Central operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 51 units and a listed service value of 54 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 44 minutes. For claim, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-08-713-07-06-36073.
