Support Procedure

Reference AUT-08-713-04-04-32420 governs the support procedure for Lumen Automotive Central. The warranty workflow supports booking and records vehicle information under the Central operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 32 units and a listed service value of 190 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 192 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-08-713-04-04-32420.
