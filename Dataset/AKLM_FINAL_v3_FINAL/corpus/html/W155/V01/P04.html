Support Procedure

Reference AUT-08-713-01-04-29393 governs the support procedure for Lumen Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports claim and records service booking information under the Central operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 86 units and a listed service value of 110 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 61 minutes. For inspection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-08-713-01-04-29393.
