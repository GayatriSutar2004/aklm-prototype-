Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-08-713-01-06-30019 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports claim and records inspection information under the Central operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 80 units and a listed service value of 315 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 144 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-08-713-01-06-30019.
