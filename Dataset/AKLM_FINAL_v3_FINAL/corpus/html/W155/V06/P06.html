Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-08-713-06-06-35064 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Automotive Central. The vehicle workflow supports claim and records vehicle information under the Central operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 69 units and a listed service value of 308 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 121 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-08-713-06-06-35064.
