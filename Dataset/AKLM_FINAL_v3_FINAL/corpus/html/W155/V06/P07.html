Timing And Scheduling

Reference AUT-08-713-06-07-35377 governs the timing and scheduling for Lumen Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports claim and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 66 units and a listed service value of 200 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 72 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-08-713-06-07-35377.
