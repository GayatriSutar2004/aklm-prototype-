Support Procedure

Reference AUT-08-713-03-04-31411 governs the support procedure for Lumen Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports booking and records part information under the Central operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 50 units and a listed service value of 444 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 88 minutes. For inspection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-08-713-03-04-31411.
