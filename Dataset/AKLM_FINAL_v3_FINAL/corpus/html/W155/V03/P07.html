Timing And Scheduling

Reference AUT-08-713-03-07-32350 governs the timing and scheduling for Lumen Automotive Central. The part workflow supports booking and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 41 units and a listed service value of 120 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 122 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-08-713-03-07-32350.
