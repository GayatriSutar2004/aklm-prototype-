Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TEL-08-639-01-06-14181 governs the eligibility and requirements for Lumen Telecom East. The support request workflow supports transfer and records bundle information under the East operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 42 units and a listed service value of 54 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 53 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TEL-08-639-01-06-14181.
