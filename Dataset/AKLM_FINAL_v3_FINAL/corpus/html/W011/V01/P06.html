Eligibility And Requirements

Reference EVE-01-370-01-06-89683 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Events North. The session workflow supports transfer and records schedule information under the North operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 30 units and a listed service value of 47 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 108 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EVE-01-370-01-06-89683.
