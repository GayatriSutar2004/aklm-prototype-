Support Procedure

Reference HEA-02-888-04-04-02950 governs the support procedure for Nimbus Healthcare Central. The appointment workflow supports verification and records appointment information under the Central operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 90 units and a listed service value of 65 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 69 minutes. For rescheduling, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-02-888-04-04-02950.
