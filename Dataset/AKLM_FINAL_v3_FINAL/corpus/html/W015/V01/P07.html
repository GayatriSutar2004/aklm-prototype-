Timing And Scheduling

Reference AUT-01-518-01-07-21672 governs the timing and scheduling for Atlas Automotive Central. The part workflow supports maintenance and records inspection information under the Central operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 24 units and a listed service value of 40 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 60 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-01-518-01-07-21672.
