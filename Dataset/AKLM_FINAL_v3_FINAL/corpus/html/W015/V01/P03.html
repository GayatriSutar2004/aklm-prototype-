Operating Policy

Reference AUT-01-518-01-03-20420 governs the operating policy for Atlas Automotive Central. The vehicle workflow supports claim and records inspection information under the Central operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 36 units and a listed service value of 51 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 75 minutes. For inspection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-01-518-01-03-20420.
