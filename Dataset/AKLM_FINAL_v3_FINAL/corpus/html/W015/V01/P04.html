Support Procedure

Reference AUT-01-518-01-04-20733 governs the support procedure for Atlas Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports claim and records service booking information under the Central operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 33 units and a listed service value of 364 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 26 minutes. For claim, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-01-518-01-04-20733.
