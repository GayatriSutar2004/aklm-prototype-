Operating Policy

Reference AUT-01-518-08-03-27483 governs the operating policy for Atlas Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports maintenance and records inspection information under the Central operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 68 units and a listed service value of 378 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 79 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-01-518-08-03-27483.
