Support Procedure

Reference AUT-01-518-08-04-27796 governs the support procedure for Atlas Automotive Central. The warranty workflow supports maintenance and records service booking information under the Central operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 65 units and a listed service value of 270 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 30 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-01-518-08-04-27796.
