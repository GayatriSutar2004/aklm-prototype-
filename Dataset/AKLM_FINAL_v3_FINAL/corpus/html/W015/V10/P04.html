Support Procedure

Reference AUT-01-518-10-04-29814 governs the support procedure for Atlas Automotive Central. The part workflow supports replacement and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 29 units and a listed service value of 183 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 57 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-01-518-10-04-29814.
