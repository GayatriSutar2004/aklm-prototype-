Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-01-518-05-06-25395 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports inspection and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 34 units and a listed service value of 395 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 163 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-01-518-05-06-25395.
