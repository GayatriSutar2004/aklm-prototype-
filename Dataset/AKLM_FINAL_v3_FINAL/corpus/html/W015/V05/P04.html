Support Procedure

Reference AUT-01-518-05-04-24769 governs the support procedure for Atlas Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports replacement and records warranty information under the Central operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 40 units and a listed service value of 190 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 80 minutes. For claim, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-01-518-05-04-24769.
