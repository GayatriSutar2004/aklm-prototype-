Support Procedure

Reference AUT-01-518-04-04-23760 governs the support procedure for Atlas Automotive Central. The part workflow supports booking and records vehicle information under the Central operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 58 units and a listed service value of 444 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 157 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-01-518-04-04-23760.
