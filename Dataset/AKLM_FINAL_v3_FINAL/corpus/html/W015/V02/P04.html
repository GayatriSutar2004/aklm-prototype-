Support Procedure

Reference AUT-01-518-02-04-21742 governs the support procedure for Atlas Automotive Central. The warranty workflow supports maintenance and records inspection information under the Central operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 15 units and a listed service value of 110 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 130 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-01-518-02-04-21742.
