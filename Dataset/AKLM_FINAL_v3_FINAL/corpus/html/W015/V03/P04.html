Support Procedure

Reference AUT-01-518-03-04-22751 governs the support procedure for Atlas Automotive Central. The inspection workflow supports booking and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 76 units and a listed service value of 277 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 53 minutes. For claim, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-01-518-03-04-22751.
