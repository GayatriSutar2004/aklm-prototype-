Operating Policy

Reference AUT-01-518-06-03-25465 governs the operating policy for Atlas Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports inspection and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 25 units and a listed service value of 44 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 52 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-01-518-06-03-25465.
