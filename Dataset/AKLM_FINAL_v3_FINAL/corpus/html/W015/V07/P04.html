Support Procedure

Reference AUT-01-518-07-04-26787 governs the support procedure for Atlas Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports claim and records vehicle information under the Central operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 83 units and a listed service value of 103 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 107 minutes. For claim, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-01-518-07-04-26787.
