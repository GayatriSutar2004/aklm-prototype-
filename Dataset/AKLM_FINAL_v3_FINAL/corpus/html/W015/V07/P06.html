Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-01-518-07-06-27413 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Automotive Central. The inspection workflow supports maintenance and records inspection information under the Central operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 77 units and a listed service value of 308 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 190 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-01-518-07-06-27413.
