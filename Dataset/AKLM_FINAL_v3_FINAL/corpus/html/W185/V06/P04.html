Support Procedure

Reference HEA-10-826-06-04-72008 governs the support procedure for Aurora Healthcare Central. The care plan workflow supports rescheduling and records appointment information under the Central operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 13 units and a listed service value of 229 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 136 minutes. For rescheduling, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-10-826-06-04-72008.
