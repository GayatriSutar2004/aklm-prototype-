Pricing And Commercial Terms

Reference UTI-02-224-03-02-72586 governs the pricing and commercial terms for Nimbus Utilities West. The meter workflow supports connection and records meter information under the West operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 48 units and a listed service value of 236 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 20 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-02-224-03-02-72586.
