Support Procedure

Reference UTI-02-224-04-04-74221 governs the support procedure for Nimbus Utilities West. The connection workflow supports reconnection and records inspection information under the West operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 24 units and a listed service value of 187 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 26 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-02-224-04-04-74221.
