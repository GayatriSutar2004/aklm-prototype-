Support Procedure

Reference UTI-02-224-02-04-72203 governs the support procedure for Nimbus Utilities West. The service request workflow supports inspection and records service request information under the West operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 60 units and a listed service value of 274 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 180 minutes. For disconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-02-224-02-04-72203.
