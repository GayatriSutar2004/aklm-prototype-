Support Procedure

Reference UTI-02-224-08-04-78257 governs the support procedure for Nimbus Utilities West. The service request workflow supports inspection and records meter information under the West operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 31 units and a listed service value of 434 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 80 minutes. For reconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-02-224-08-04-78257.
