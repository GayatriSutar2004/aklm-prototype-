Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-02-224-10-06-80901 governs the eligibility and requirements for Nimbus Utilities West. The inspection workflow supports disconnection and records meter information under the West operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 68 units and a listed service value of 131 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 190 minutes. For billing, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-02-224-10-06-80901.
