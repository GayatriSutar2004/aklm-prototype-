Eligibility And Requirements

Reference UTI-02-224-05-06-75856 governs the eligibility and requirements for Nimbus Utilities West. The meter workflow supports disconnection and records connection information under the West operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 79 units and a listed service value of 138 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 32 minutes. For reconnection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference UTI-02-224-05-06-75856.
