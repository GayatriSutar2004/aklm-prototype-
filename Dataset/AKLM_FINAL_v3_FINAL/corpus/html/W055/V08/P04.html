Support Procedure

Reference AUT-03-004-08-04-44556 governs the support procedure for Vertex Automotive Central. The part workflow supports inspection and records part information under the Central operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 35 units and a listed service value of 438 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 40 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-03-004-08-04-44556.
