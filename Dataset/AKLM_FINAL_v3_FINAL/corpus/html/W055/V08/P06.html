Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-03-004-08-06-45182 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Automotive Central. The vehicle workflow supports claim and records service booking information under the Central operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 29 units and a listed service value of 222 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 123 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-03-004-08-06-45182.
