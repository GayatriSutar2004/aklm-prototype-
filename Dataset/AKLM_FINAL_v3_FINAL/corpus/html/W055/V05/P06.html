Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-03-004-05-06-42155 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Automotive Central. The inspection workflow supports booking and records service booking information under the Central operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 83 units and a listed service value of 142 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 173 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-03-004-05-06-42155.
