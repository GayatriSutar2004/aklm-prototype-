Support Procedure

Reference AUT-03-004-05-04-41529 governs the support procedure for Vertex Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports booking and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 89 units and a listed service value of 358 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 90 minutes. For inspection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-03-004-05-04-41529.
