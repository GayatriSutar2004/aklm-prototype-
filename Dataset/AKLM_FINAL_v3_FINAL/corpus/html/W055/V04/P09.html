Available Services

Reference AUT-03-004-04-09-42085 governs the available services for Vertex Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports booking and records service booking information under the Central operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 13 units and a listed service value of 72 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 103 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-03-004-04-09-42085.
