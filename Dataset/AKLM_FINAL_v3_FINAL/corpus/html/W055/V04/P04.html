Support Procedure

Reference AUT-03-004-04-04-40520 governs the support procedure for Vertex Automotive Central. The vehicle workflow supports maintenance and records inspection information under the Central operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 28 units and a listed service value of 191 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 167 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-03-004-04-04-40520.
