Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-03-004-04-06-41146 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Automotive Central. The warranty workflow supports booking and records vehicle information under the Central operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 22 units and a listed service value of 396 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 69 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-03-004-04-06-41146.
