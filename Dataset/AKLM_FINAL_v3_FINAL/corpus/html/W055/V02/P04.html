Support Procedure

Reference AUT-03-004-02-04-38502 governs the support procedure for Vertex Automotive Central. The part workflow supports inspection and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 64 units and a listed service value of 278 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 140 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-03-004-02-04-38502.
