Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-03-004-02-06-39128 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Automotive Central. The vehicle workflow supports claim and records warranty information under the Central operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 58 units and a listed service value of 62 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 42 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-03-004-02-06-39128.
