Support Procedure

Reference AUT-03-004-10-04-46574 governs the support procedure for Vertex Automotive Central. The vehicle workflow supports maintenance and records service booking information under the Central operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 78 units and a listed service value of 351 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 67 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-03-004-10-04-46574.
