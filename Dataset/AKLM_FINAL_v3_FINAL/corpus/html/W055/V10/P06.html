Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-03-004-10-06-47200 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Automotive Central. The warranty workflow supports booking and records part information under the Central operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 72 units and a listed service value of 135 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 150 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-03-004-10-06-47200.
