Pricing And Commercial Terms

Reference AUT-03-004-07-02-42921 governs the pricing and commercial terms for Vertex Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports replacement and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 59 units and a listed service value of 66 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 34 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-03-004-07-02-42921.
