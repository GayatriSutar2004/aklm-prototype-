Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-03-004-07-06-44173 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports inspection and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 47 units and a listed service value of 55 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 200 minutes. For claim, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-03-004-07-06-44173.
