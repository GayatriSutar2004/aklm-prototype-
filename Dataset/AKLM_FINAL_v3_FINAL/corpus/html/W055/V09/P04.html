Support Procedure

Reference AUT-03-004-09-04-45565 governs the support procedure for Vertex Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports claim and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 17 units and a listed service value of 184 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 144 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-03-004-09-04-45565.
