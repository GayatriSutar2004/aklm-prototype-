Security And Access

Reference AUT-03-004-01-08-38745 governs the security and access for Vertex Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports claim and records inspection information under the Central operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 70 units and a listed service value of 100 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 21 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-03-004-01-08-38745.
