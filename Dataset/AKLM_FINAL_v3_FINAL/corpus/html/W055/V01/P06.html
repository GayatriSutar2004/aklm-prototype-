Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-03-004-01-06-38119 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports inspection and records service booking information under the Central operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 76 units and a listed service value of 316 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 119 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-03-004-01-06-38119.
