Support Procedure

Reference AUT-03-004-01-04-37493 governs the support procedure for Vertex Automotive Central. The inspection workflow supports replacement and records part information under the Central operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 82 units and a listed service value of 111 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 36 minutes. For inspection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-03-004-01-04-37493.
