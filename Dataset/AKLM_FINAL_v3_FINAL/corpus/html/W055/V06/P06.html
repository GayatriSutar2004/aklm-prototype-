Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-03-004-06-06-43164 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Automotive Central. The part workflow supports replacement and records inspection information under the Central operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 65 units and a listed service value of 309 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 96 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-03-004-06-06-43164.
