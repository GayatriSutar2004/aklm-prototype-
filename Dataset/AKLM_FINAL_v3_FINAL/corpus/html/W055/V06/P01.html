Service Overview

Reference AUT-03-004-06-01-41599 governs the service overview for Vertex Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports booking and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 80 units and a listed service value of 428 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 160 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-03-004-06-01-41599.
