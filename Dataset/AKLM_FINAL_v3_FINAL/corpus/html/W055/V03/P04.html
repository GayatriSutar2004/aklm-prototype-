Support Procedure

Reference AUT-03-004-03-04-39511 governs the support procedure for Vertex Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports claim and records service booking information under the Central operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 46 units and a listed service value of 445 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 63 minutes. For inspection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-03-004-03-04-39511.
