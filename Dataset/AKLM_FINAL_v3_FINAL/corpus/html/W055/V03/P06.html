Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-03-004-03-06-40137 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports maintenance and records part information under the Central operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 40 units and a listed service value of 229 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 146 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-03-004-03-06-40137.
