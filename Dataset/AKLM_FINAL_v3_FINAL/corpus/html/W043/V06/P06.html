Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-03-557-06-06-48136 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Travel East. The boarding pass workflow supports modification and records boarding pass information under the East operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 74 units and a listed service value of 427 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 93 minutes. For transfer, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-03-557-06-06-48136.
