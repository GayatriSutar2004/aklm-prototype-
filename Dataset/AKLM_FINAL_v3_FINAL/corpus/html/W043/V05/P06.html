Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-03-557-05-06-47127 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Travel East. The transfer workflow supports transfer and records transfer information under the East operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 13 units and a listed service value of 260 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 170 minutes. For check-in, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-03-557-05-06-47127.
