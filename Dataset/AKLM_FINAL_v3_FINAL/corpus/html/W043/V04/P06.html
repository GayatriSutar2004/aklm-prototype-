Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-03-557-04-06-46118 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Travel East. The luggage workflow supports booking and records itinerary information under the East operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 31 units and a listed service value of 93 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 66 minutes. For transfer, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-03-557-04-06-46118.
