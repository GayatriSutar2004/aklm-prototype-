Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-03-557-08-06-50154 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Travel East. The reservation workflow supports check-in and records luggage information under the East operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 38 units and a listed service value of 340 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 120 minutes. For transfer, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-03-557-08-06-50154.
