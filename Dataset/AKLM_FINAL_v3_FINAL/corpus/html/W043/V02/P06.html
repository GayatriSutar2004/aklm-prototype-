Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-03-557-02-06-44100 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Travel East. The reservation workflow supports check-in and records transfer information under the East operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 67 units and a listed service value of 180 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 39 minutes. For transfer, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-03-557-02-06-44100.
