Eligibility And Requirements

Reference LOG-07-084-06-06-00441 governs the eligibility and requirements for Pioneer Logistics Highland. The route workflow supports pickup and records pickup information under the Highland operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 62 units and a listed service value of 405 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 162 minutes. For rerouting, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference LOG-07-084-06-06-00441.
