Operating Policy

Reference AUT-06-230-10-03-21401 governs the operating policy for Harbor Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports inspection and records inspection information under the Central operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 36 units and a listed service value of 290 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 131 minutes. For claim, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-06-230-10-03-21401.
