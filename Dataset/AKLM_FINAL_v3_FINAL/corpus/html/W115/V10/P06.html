Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-06-230-10-06-22340 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Automotive Central. The warranty workflow supports claim and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 27 units and a listed service value of 387 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 165 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-06-230-10-06-22340.
