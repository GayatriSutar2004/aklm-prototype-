Available Services

Reference AUT-06-230-10-09-23279 governs the available services for Harbor Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports claim and records vehicle information under the Central operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 18 units and a listed service value of 63 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 199 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-06-230-10-09-23279.
