Support Procedure

Reference AUT-06-230-10-04-21714 governs the support procedure for Harbor Automotive Central. The vehicle workflow supports inspection and records warranty information under the Central operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 33 units and a listed service value of 182 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 82 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-06-230-10-04-21714.
