Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-06-230-09-06-21331 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports inspection and records inspection information under the Central operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 45 units and a listed service value of 220 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 61 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-06-230-09-06-21331.
