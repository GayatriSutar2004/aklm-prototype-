Support Procedure

Reference AUT-06-230-08-04-19696 governs the support procedure for Harbor Automotive Central. The part workflow supports booking and records part information under the Central operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 69 units and a listed service value of 269 units. The scheduled operating point is Tuesday at 08:00, with a standard handling duration of 55 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-06-230-08-04-19696.
