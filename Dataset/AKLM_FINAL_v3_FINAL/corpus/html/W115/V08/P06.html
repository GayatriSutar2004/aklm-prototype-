Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-06-230-08-06-20322 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Automotive Central. The vehicle workflow supports replacement and records service booking information under the Central operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 63 units and a listed service value of 53 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 138 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-06-230-08-06-20322.
