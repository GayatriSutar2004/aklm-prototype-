Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-06-230-03-06-15277 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports inspection and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 74 units and a listed service value of 60 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 161 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-06-230-03-06-15277.
