Support Procedure

Reference AUT-06-230-03-04-14651 governs the support procedure for Harbor Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports replacement and records warranty information under the Central operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 80 units and a listed service value of 276 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 78 minutes. For claim, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-06-230-03-04-14651.
