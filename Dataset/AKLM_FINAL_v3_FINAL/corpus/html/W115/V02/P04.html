Support Procedure

Reference AUT-06-230-02-04-13642 governs the support procedure for Harbor Automotive Central. The part workflow supports booking and records vehicle information under the Central operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 19 units and a listed service value of 109 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 155 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-06-230-02-04-13642.
