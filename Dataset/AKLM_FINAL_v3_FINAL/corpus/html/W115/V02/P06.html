Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-06-230-02-06-14268 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Automotive Central. The vehicle workflow supports replacement and records inspection information under the Central operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 13 units and a listed service value of 314 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 57 minutes. For inspection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-06-230-02-06-14268.
