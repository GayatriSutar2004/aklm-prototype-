Support Procedure

Reference AUT-06-230-06-04-17678 governs the support procedure for Harbor Automotive Central. The warranty workflow supports maintenance and records service booking information under the Central operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 26 units and a listed service value of 356 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 28 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-06-230-06-04-17678.
