Pricing And Commercial Terms

Reference AUT-06-230-01-02-12007 governs the pricing and commercial terms for Harbor Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports maintenance and records service booking information under the Central operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 43 units and a listed service value of 158 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 149 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-06-230-01-02-12007.
