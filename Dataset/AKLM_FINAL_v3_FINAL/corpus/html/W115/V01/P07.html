Timing And Scheduling

Reference AUT-06-230-01-07-13572 governs the timing and scheduling for Harbor Automotive Central. The vehicle workflow supports booking and records vehicle information under the Central operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 28 units and a listed service value of 39 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 85 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-06-230-01-07-13572.
