Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-06-230-01-06-13259 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports booking and records service booking information under the Central operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 31 units and a listed service value of 147 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 134 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-06-230-01-06-13259.
