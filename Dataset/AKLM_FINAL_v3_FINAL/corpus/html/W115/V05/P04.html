Support Procedure

Reference AUT-06-230-05-04-16669 governs the support procedure for Harbor Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports claim and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 44 units and a listed service value of 189 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 105 minutes. For inspection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-06-230-05-04-16669.
