Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-06-230-04-06-16286 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Automotive Central. The warranty workflow supports inspection and records vehicle information under the Central operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 56 units and a listed service value of 227 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 84 minutes. For inspection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-06-230-04-06-16286.
