Operating Policy

Reference AUT-06-230-04-03-15347 governs the operating policy for Harbor Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports inspection and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 65 units and a listed service value of 130 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 50 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-06-230-04-03-15347.
