Support Procedure

Reference AUT-06-230-04-04-15660 governs the support procedure for Harbor Automotive Central. The vehicle workflow supports inspection and records inspection information under the Central operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 62 units and a listed service value of 443 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 182 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-06-230-04-04-15660.
