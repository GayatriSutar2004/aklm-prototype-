Support Procedure

Reference AUT-06-230-07-04-18687 governs the support procedure for Harbor Automotive Central. The inspection workflow supports maintenance and records inspection information under the Central operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 87 units and a listed service value of 102 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 132 minutes. For inspection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-06-230-07-04-18687.
