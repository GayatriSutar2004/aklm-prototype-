Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-06-230-07-06-19313 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports booking and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 81 units and a listed service value of 307 units. The scheduled operating point is Thursday at 10:00, with a standard handling duration of 34 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-06-230-07-06-19313.
