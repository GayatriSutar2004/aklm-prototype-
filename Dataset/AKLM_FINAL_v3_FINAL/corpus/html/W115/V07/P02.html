Pricing And Commercial Terms

Reference AUT-06-230-07-02-18061 governs the pricing and commercial terms for Harbor Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports maintenance and records vehicle information under the Central operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 14 units and a listed service value of 318 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 49 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-06-230-07-02-18061.
