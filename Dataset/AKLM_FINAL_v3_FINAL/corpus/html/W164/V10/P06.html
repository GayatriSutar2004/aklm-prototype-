Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HOS-09-049-10-06-10371 governs the eligibility and requirements for Summit Hospitality West. The reservation workflow supports check-in and records room information under the West operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 89 units and a listed service value of 256 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 132 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HOS-09-049-10-06-10371.
