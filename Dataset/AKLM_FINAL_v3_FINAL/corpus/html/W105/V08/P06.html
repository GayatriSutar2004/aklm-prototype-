Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HEA-06-857-08-06-41132 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Healthcare Central. The care plan workflow supports rescheduling and records session information under the Central operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 31 units and a listed service value of 432 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 45 minutes. For verification, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-06-857-08-06-41132.
