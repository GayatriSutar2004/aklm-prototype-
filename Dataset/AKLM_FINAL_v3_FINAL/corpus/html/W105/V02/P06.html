Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HEA-06-857-02-06-35078 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Healthcare Central. The care plan workflow supports rescheduling and records care plan information under the Central operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 60 units and a listed service value of 272 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 145 minutes. For rescheduling, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-06-857-02-06-35078.
