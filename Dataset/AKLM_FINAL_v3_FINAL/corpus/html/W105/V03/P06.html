Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HEA-06-857-03-06-36087 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Healthcare Central. The referral workflow supports rescheduling and records referral information under the Central operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 42 units and a listed service value of 439 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 68 minutes. For review, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-06-857-03-06-36087.
