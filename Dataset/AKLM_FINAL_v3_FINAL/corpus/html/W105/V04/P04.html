Support Procedure

Reference HEA-06-857-04-04-36470 governs the support procedure for Harbor Healthcare Central. The care plan workflow supports booking and records care plan information under the Central operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 30 units and a listed service value of 401 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 89 minutes. For cancellation, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-06-857-04-04-36470.
