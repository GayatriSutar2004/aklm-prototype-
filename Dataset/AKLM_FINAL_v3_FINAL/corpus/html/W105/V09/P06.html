Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HEA-06-857-09-06-42141 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Healthcare Central. The referral workflow supports booking and records care plan information under the Central operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 13 units and a listed service value of 178 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 149 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HEA-06-857-09-06-42141.
