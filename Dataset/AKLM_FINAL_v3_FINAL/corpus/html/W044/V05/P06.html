Eligibility And Requirements

Reference HOS-03-594-05-06-55046 governs the eligibility and requirements for Vertex Hospitality West. The guest workflow supports check-in and records guest information under the West operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 32 units and a listed service value of 180 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 125 minutes. For checkout, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference HOS-03-594-05-06-55046.
