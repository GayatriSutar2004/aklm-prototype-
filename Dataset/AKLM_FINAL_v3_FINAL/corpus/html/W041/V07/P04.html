Support Procedure

Reference EDU-03-483-07-04-32681 governs the support procedure for Vertex Education North. The certificate workflow supports enrolment and records scholarship information under the North operating profile. Version 7 specifies a threshold of 24 units and a listed service value of 128 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 23 minutes. For enrolment, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EDU-03-483-07-04-32681.
