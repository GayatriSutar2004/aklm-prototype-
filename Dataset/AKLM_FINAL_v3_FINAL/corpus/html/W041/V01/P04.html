Support Procedure

Reference EDU-03-483-01-04-26627 governs the support procedure for Vertex Education North. The certificate workflow supports withdrawal and records certificate information under the North operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 53 units and a listed service value of 389 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 123 minutes. For enrolment, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EDU-03-483-01-04-26627.
