Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PRO-06-968-10-06-66907 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Property Highland. The maintenance request workflow supports maintenance and records deposit information under the Highland operating profile. Version 10 specifies a threshold of 52 units and a listed service value of 105 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 118 minutes. For application, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PRO-06-968-10-06-66907.
