Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PRO-06-968-01-06-57826 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Property Highland. The listing workflow supports inspection and records inspection information under the Highland operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 56 units and a listed service value of 286 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 87 minutes. For inspection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PRO-06-968-01-06-57826.
