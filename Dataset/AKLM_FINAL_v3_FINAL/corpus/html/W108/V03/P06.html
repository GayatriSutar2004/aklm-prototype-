Eligibility And Requirements

Reference PRO-06-968-03-06-59844 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Property Highland. The inspection workflow supports handover and records deposit information under the Highland operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 20 units and a listed service value of 199 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 114 minutes. For inspection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference PRO-06-968-03-06-59844.
