Eligibility And Requirements

Reference EDU-01-000-08-06-17556 governs the eligibility and requirements for Atlas Education North. The course workflow supports submission and records course information under the North operating profile. Version 8 specifies a threshold of 30 units and a listed service value of 332 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 200 minutes. For enrolment, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EDU-01-000-08-06-17556.
