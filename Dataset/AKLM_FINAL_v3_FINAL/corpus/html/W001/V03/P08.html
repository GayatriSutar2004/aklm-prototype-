Security And Access

Reference EDU-01-000-03-08-13137 governs the security and access for Atlas Education North. The scholarship workflow supports withdrawal and records course information under the North operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 35 units and a listed service value of 123 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 125 minutes. For enrolment, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EDU-01-000-03-08-13137.
