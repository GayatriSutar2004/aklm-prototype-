Support Procedure

Reference EDU-01-000-06-04-14912 governs the support procedure for Atlas Education North. The assessment workflow supports enrolment and records certificate information under the North operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 72 units and a listed service value of 214 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 90 minutes. For attendance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference EDU-01-000-06-04-14912.
