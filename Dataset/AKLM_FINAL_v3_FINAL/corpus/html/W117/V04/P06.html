Eligibility And Requirements

Reference RES-06-304-04-06-32124 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Research Metro. The dataset workflow supports submission and records dataset information under the Metro operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 15 units and a listed service value of 67 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 175 minutes. For review, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference RES-06-304-04-06-32124.
