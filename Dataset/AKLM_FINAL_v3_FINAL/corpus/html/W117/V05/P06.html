Eligibility And Requirements

Reference RES-06-304-05-06-33133 governs the eligibility and requirements for Harbor Research Metro. The study workflow supports revision and records submission information under the Metro operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 76 units and a listed service value of 234 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 98 minutes. For revision, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference RES-06-304-05-06-33133.
