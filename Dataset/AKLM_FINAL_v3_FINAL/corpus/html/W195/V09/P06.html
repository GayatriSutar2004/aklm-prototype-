Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-10-199-09-06-54851 governs the eligibility and requirements for Aurora Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports maintenance and records warranty information under the Central operating profile. Version 9 specifies a threshold of 64 units and a listed service value of 135 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 81 minutes. For inspection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-10-199-09-06-54851.
