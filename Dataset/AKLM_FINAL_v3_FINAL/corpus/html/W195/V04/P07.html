Timing And Scheduling

Reference AUT-10-199-04-07-50119 governs the timing and scheduling for Aurora Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports booking and records inspection information under the Central operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 72 units and a listed service value of 455 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 55 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-10-199-04-07-50119.
