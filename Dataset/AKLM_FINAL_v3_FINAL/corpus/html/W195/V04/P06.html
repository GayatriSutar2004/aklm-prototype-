Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-10-199-04-06-49806 governs the eligibility and requirements for Aurora Automotive Central. The vehicle workflow supports booking and records roadside request information under the Central operating profile. Version 4 specifies a threshold of 75 units and a listed service value of 142 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 104 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-10-199-04-06-49806.
