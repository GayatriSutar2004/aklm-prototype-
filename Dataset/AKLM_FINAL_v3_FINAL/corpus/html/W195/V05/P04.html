Support Procedure

Reference AUT-10-199-05-04-50189 governs the support procedure for Aurora Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports booking and records part information under the Central operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 63 units and a listed service value of 104 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 125 minutes. For booking, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-10-199-05-04-50189.
