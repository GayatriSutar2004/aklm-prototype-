Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-10-199-05-06-50815 governs the eligibility and requirements for Aurora Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports booking and records service booking information under the Central operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 57 units and a listed service value of 309 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 27 minutes. For inspection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-10-199-05-06-50815.
