Timing And Scheduling

Reference AUT-10-199-02-07-48101 governs the timing and scheduling for Aurora Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports claim and records vehicle information under the Central operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 29 units and a listed service value of 121 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 28 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-10-199-02-07-48101.
