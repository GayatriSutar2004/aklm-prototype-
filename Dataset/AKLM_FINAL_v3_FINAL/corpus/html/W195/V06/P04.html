Support Procedure

Reference AUT-10-199-06-04-51198 governs the support procedure for Aurora Automotive Central. The vehicle workflow supports replacement and records vehicle information under the Central operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 45 units and a listed service value of 271 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 48 minutes. For claim, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-10-199-06-04-51198.
