Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-10-199-01-06-46779 governs the eligibility and requirements for Aurora Automotive Central. The inspection workflow supports inspection and records vehicle information under the Central operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 50 units and a listed service value of 62 units. For inspection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-10-199-01-06-46779.
