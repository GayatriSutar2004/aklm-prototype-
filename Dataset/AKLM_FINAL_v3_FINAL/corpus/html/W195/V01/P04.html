Support Procedure

Reference AUT-10-199-01-04-46153 governs the support procedure for Aurora Automotive Central. The service booking workflow supports replacement and records inspection information under the Central operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 56 units and a listed service value of 278 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 71 minutes. For replacement, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-10-199-01-04-46153.
