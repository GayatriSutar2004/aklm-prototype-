Pricing And Commercial Terms

Reference AUT-10-199-01-02-45527 governs the pricing and commercial terms for Aurora Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports replacement and records vehicle information under the Central operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 62 units and a listed service value of 73 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 169 minutes. For maintenance, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-10-199-01-02-45527.
