Eligibility And Requirements

Reference AUT-10-199-03-06-48797 governs the eligibility and requirements for Aurora Automotive Central. The roadside request workflow supports maintenance and records inspection information under the Central operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 14 units and a listed service value of 396 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 181 minutes. For inspection, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference AUT-10-199-03-06-48797.
