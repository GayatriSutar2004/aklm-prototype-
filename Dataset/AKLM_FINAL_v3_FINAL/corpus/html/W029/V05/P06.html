Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-02-039-05-06-36261 governs the eligibility and requirements for Nimbus Ecommerce River. The invoice workflow supports return and records cart information under the River operating profile. Version 5 specifies a threshold of 63 units and a listed service value of 117 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 76 minutes. For delivery, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-02-039-05-06-36261.
