Support Procedure

Reference ECO-02-039-01-04-31599 governs the support procedure for Nimbus Ecommerce River. The invoice workflow supports purchase and records order information under the River operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 62 units and a listed service value of 86 units. The scheduled operating point is Friday at 11:00, with a standard handling duration of 120 minutes. For purchase, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-02-039-01-04-31599.
