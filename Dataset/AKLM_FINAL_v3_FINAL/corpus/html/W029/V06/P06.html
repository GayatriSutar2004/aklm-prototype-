Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-02-039-06-06-37270 governs the eligibility and requirements for Nimbus Ecommerce River. The order workflow supports purchase and records order information under the River operating profile. Version 6 specifies a threshold of 45 units and a listed service value of 284 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 180 minutes. For return, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-02-039-06-06-37270.
