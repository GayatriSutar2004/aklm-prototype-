Eligibility And Requirements

Reference ECO-02-039-03-06-34243 governs the eligibility and requirements for Nimbus Ecommerce River. The shipment workflow supports delivery and records order information under the River operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 20 units and a listed service value of 204 units. The scheduled operating point is Wednesday at 09:00, with a standard handling duration of 49 minutes. For delivery, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference ECO-02-039-03-06-34243.
