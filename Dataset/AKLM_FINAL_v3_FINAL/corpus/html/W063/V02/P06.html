Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-04-300-02-06-02480 governs the eligibility and requirements for Cedar Travel East. The boarding pass workflow supports check-in and records luggage information under the East operating profile. Version 2 specifies a threshold of 52 units and a listed service value of 264 units. The scheduled operating point is Sunday at 13:00, with a standard handling duration of 44 minutes. For check-in, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-04-300-02-06-02480.
