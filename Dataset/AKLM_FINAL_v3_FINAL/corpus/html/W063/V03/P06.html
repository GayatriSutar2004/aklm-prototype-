Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-04-300-03-06-03489 governs the eligibility and requirements for Cedar Travel East. The route workflow supports check-in and records transfer information under the East operating profile. Version 3 specifies a threshold of 34 units and a listed service value of 431 units. The scheduled operating point is Monday at 07:00, with a standard handling duration of 148 minutes. For transfer, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-04-300-03-06-03489.
