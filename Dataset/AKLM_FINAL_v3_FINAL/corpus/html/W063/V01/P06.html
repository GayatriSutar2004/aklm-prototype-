Eligibility And Requirements

Reference TRA-04-300-01-06-01471 governs the eligibility and requirements for Cedar Travel East. The transfer workflow supports modification and records reservation information under the East operating profile. Version 1 specifies a threshold of 70 units and a listed service value of 97 units. The scheduled operating point is Saturday at 12:00, with a standard handling duration of 121 minutes. For transfer, the requester follows the site-specific verification route associated with reference TRA-04-300-01-06-01471.
